## How the names were checked

On 2026-10-31 each name was searched on the open web in quotes, with the town or "Tanzania". A name is "clear" when no business with that name turned up. This only checks what is online. It is not a BRELA or Zanzibar (BPRA) register search, and small businesses often have no web presence. Before the seed goes live, legal can say whether a register check is worth doing.

One name was dropped. "Kilele Garden Hotel" (Moshi) was too close to Kileleni Lodge, also called Kilele Lodge, a real place in Moshi. It was replaced by "Mpera Court Hotel".

## The listings

Opening hours below are invented for the sample, written the way an owner would write them.

### 1. Mpera Court Hotel (hotel)
- Region: Kilimanjaro
- Location: a quiet side street a short drive from Moshi town centre, with a view of Kibo on clear mornings
- Story: A small family-run hotel built around an old guava tree in the courtyard. Climbers often stay the night before and after the mountain, and the staff can store bags while guests are on the trek. Breakfast is served under the tree.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili
- Hours: reception open 24 hours; breakfast 6:30 to 10:00

### 2. Pwani Lantern Hotel (hotel)
- Region: Dar es Salaam
- Location: near the ocean road, a few minutes from the city centre and the Zanzibar ferry terminal
- Story: A mid-size city hotel for people passing through Dar on the way to Zanzibar or the south. Rooms are simple and quiet, and the lobby has paper lanterns made by a local craft group. Staff can help book ferry tickets at the official counter.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, French
- Hours: reception open 24 hours; check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00

### 3. Mto Kijivu Tented Camp (lodge)
- Region: Arusha
- Location: on the edge of farmland between Mto wa Mbu and Karatu, on the way to Lake Manyara and Ngorongoro
- Story: Twelve canvas tents on raised wooden floors beside a seasonal river. The camp is a stop for guests heading to Ngorongoro, with early breakfast for crater departures. Evenings are spent around the fire.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, German
- Hours: open all year; early breakfast from 5:30 on request

### 4. Shamba la Ndizi Homestay (homestay)
- Region: Kilimanjaro
- Location: a village on the lower slopes of Kilimanjaro, above Moshi, among banana and coffee plots
- Story: A Chagga family hosts up to four guests in two rooms of their home. Guests can join the day's work in the shamba, learn to cook mtori (banana soup) and walk to a nearby waterfall with a local guide. Meals are eaten with the family.
- Languages: Kiswahili, English
- Hours: arrivals between 9:00 and 18:00; dinner at about 19:30

### 5. Kahawa Bunk Hostel (hostel)
- Region: Arusha
- Location: a walkable street near Arusha town centre and the clock tower
- Story: A backpacker hostel with dorm bunks and a few private rooms, popular with people joining group safaris. The kitchen is shared, there is a board where travellers look for others to share a safari, and coffee from nearby farms is on all day.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, Spanish
- Hours: reception 7:00 to 23:00; late arrivals by message ahead

### 6. Mnara Loft Apartments (rental)
- Region: Zanzibar (Mjini Magharibi)
- Location: inside Stone Town, a short walk from the seafront and the old market
- Story: Two self-catering apartments on the top floors of an old coral-stone house, with carved wooden doors and a shared roof terrace. Each has a small kitchen, so guests can cook what they buy at the market. The host lives nearby and meets guests at the door.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, Italian
- Hours: self check-in from 14:00 by arrangement; host reachable 8:00 to 21:00

### 7. Kinyago Lane Crafts (curio_shop)
- Region: Arusha
- Location: a small shop in Arusha town, a few streets from the Maasai market
- Story: A small shop that sells work from carvers, painters and beaders in and around Arusha. Each piece has a tag with the maker's first name and where they work. The shop can wrap pieces for flying and deliver to hotels in town.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili
- Hours: Monday to Saturday 9:00 to 18:30; Sunday 10:00 to 15:00

### 8. Zambarau Gem House (gem_shop)
- Region: Arusha
- Location: Arusha town centre, on a main shopping street
- Story: A small gem shop selling loose tanzanite and other East African stones, plus silver and gold settings. Staff explain colour, cut and size before any sale. Zambarau is the Kiswahili name for the purple java plum.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, German
- Hours: Monday to Saturday 9:00 to 18:00; closed Sunday

### 9. Mguu Imara Gear Hire (gear_rental)
- Region: Kilimanjaro
- Location: Moshi town, near the main road to the Kilimanjaro park gates
- Story: A rental shop for Kilimanjaro and Meru climbers who don't want to fly with heavy kit. All gear is washed and checked between climbs, and sizes can be tried on in the shop the day before the trek. Mguu imara means "strong leg".
- Languages: English, Kiswahili
- Hours: every day 8:00 to 19:00; gear returns until 21:00 by arrangement

### 10. Mshale Shuttle & Transfers (transport)
- Region: Kilimanjaro
- Location: based near Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO); runs to Moshi and Arusha
- Story: A small fleet of minivans for airport pickups and town-to-town rides between Moshi and Arusha. Drivers wait with a name sign and track flight delays. Mshale means "arrow".
- Languages: English, Kiswahili
- Hours: pickups for every scheduled JRO flight; bookings by message 6:00 to 22:00

### 11. Gurudumu Car Hire (car_hire)
- Region: Dar es Salaam
- Location: office near the airport road, with handover at the airport or a hotel in the city
- Story: Saloon cars and 4x4s with or without a driver, for city trips and drives south. Every car is shown to the customer on handover, with photos taken together. Gurudumu means "wheel".
- Languages: English, Kiswahili
- Hours: office Monday to Saturday 8:00 to 18:00; airport handovers any time by booking

### 12. Ukwaju Kitchen (restaurant)
- Region: Kilimanjaro
- Location: Moshi town, on a street with other cafes, a few minutes from the bus stand
- Story: A small restaurant cooking everyday Tanzanian dishes: pilau, wali na maharage, nyama choma on Fridays, and fresh juice with tamarind (ukwaju). The kitchen is open to view, and there is always a vegetarian plate.
- Languages: Kiswahili, English
- Hours: Monday to Saturday 7:00 to 22:00; Sunday 12:00 to 21:00

### 13. Mvinje Calm Spa (spa)
- Region: Zanzibar (Kusini, south-east coast)
- Location: a short walk from the beach in a south-east coast village, shaded by casuarina trees (mvinje)
- Story: A small spa with three treatment rooms, offering massages with coconut oil and clove-scented scrubs. Booking ahead is best in high season. Therapists ask about health conditions before every treatment.
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, Italian
- Hours: every day 9:00 to 20:00

### 14. Msuko Studio (salon)
- Region: Arusha
- Location: Arusha town, upstairs from a row of shops
- Story: A hair studio for braids (msuko), cornrows, twists and short cuts, with a barber's chair for beard trims. Long braiding styles take a few hours, so staff give a time estimate before starting. Travellers are welcome without an appointment on weekdays.
- Languages: Kiswahili, English
- Hours: Monday to Saturday 8:30 to 20:00; Sunday 11:00 to 18:00

### 15. Mwezi Rooftop Lounge (nightlife)
- Region: Dar es Salaam
- Location: on the roof of a building in a busy evening area of the city, with a view towards the ocean
- Story: A rooftop bar with bongo flava and amapiano DJs at weekends and a quieter acoustic night midweek. There are light snacks and soft drinks as well as a bar. Mwezi means "moon".
- Languages: English, Kiswahili
- Hours: Wednesday to Sunday 17:00 to late; closed Monday and Tuesday

### 16. Mdalasini Cooking Class (other)
- Region: Zanzibar (Mjini Magharibi)
- Location: a family home on the edge of Stone Town; the class starts with a walk through the market
- Story: A half-day class for small groups that starts by buying spices and fish at the market and ends with lunch the guests have cooked. Dishes change with the season, for example pilau, coconut fish curry and chapati. Mdalasini means "cinnamon".
- Languages: English, Kiswahili, French
- Hours: classes at 9:00 and 14:00, Monday to Saturday
