## 1. VIP "personal trip host"

### 1.1 What the host would do (proposed scope for the advocate to test)

Plans the day with the traveller, accompanies them, explains places, books restaurants or activities **in the traveller's own name**, coordinates with licensed providers already on Kwetuly, and is the traveller's single point of contact. **Not included:** driving the traveller for pay (transfers off), protection or security, handling the traveller's money beyond the host's own fee, selling packages of third-party services under the host's own name.

### 1.2 Licences that may apply

| Item | What the sources say | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guide registration | Tour guides must be registered (Tourism Act, 2008, Part VII, reported s. 42; Tourism (Tour Guides) Regulations, 2015, GN 270 of 2015). Reported conditions: Tanzanian citizen, 21+, secondary education, first-aid certificate, and **"enlisted under a licensed tour company, Tanzania Tour Guide Association or similar established entity"**. Unregistered guiding: fine of at least TZS 1 million, up to 6 months, or both. | [GN 270 of 2015, TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/2015/270/eng@2015-07-17) [Official, wording from a search summary]; [FB Attorneys](https://fbattorneys.co.tz/registration-of-tour-guides/) [Secondary]. Already in travel-agent-and-transfers.md 2.4 |
| Citizens only | "Tour guiding within Tanzania" is on the list of activities barred to non-citizens (GN 487A of 2025). So the VIP host must be a Tanzanian citizen; a foreign "host" living in Tanzania is not possible. | [DLA Piper / IMMMA, Aug 2025](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/tanzania/insights/2025/A29574-legal-alert-flyer-opt1-v1) [Secondary] |
| MNRT tour operator / travel agent | Class A covers tour operator, travel agent, car hire and mountain climbing. Travel agent: "engaged in the sale of professional services to travelers". Travel agent and car hire licences reported open only to companies fully owned by Tanzanians. | [TISEZA: Tourism licences](https://procedures.tiseza.go.tz/objective/247?l=en) [Official summary page] |
| **Where the line is** [Unverified, my reading] | Accompanying and explaining = guiding (registration). **Selling** a bundled itinerary (hotel + car + park + host) for one price, or reselling other providers' services with a margin, looks like travel agent or tour operator work, which an individual guide probably cannot be licensed for. Booking in the traveller's name, with the traveller paying each provider directly, probably stays inside guiding. **Advocate to confirm.** | - |
| Enlistment | If the enlistment condition means a guide may not contract with travellers on their own account, a VIP host would have to work under a licensed tour company or TTGA. Same open question as travel-agent-and-transfers.md question 13. | [Unverified] |
| National parks | Entering parks or the Ngorongoro area with a traveller brings in TANAPA / NCAA guide and vehicle rules. | Not researched |

### 1.3 Close protection and private security: excluded

- **Kwetuly decision:** no bodyguard, close protection, escort-for-safety or "security" wording in any host service, title, tag or description. Words like "protection", "security", "safe escort", "bodyguard" are blocked in host texts.
- **What I found about the law:** I could **not** find a dedicated private security licensing law for Tanzania. The Police Force and Auxiliary Services Act (Cap. 322) covers the police, the Police Reserve and Auxiliary Police; reports say private security companies follow guidelines issued by the Police Force. An academic study describes the registration and licensing rules as loosely drawn. The Tanzania Bureau of Standards has a draft code of practice for manned security services. Carrying firearms is separately controlled (Firearms and Ammunition Control Act, 2015, **to verify**).
  - [Police Force and Auxiliary Services Act, TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/ord/1939/1) [Official, not read]
  - [The Citizen: police and private security companies](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/police-call-for-stronger-collaboration-with-private-security-companies-5517404) [Secondary]
  - [AllAfrica, Mar 2026: "Strictly hire trained guards"](https://allafrica.com/stories/202603100101.html) [Secondary]
  - [TBS draft code of practice for manned security services (PDF)](https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1619079049-EDC%204%20(412)%20DTZS%20Code%20of%20practice%20for%20manned%20security%20services.pdf) [Official draft standard, not read]
  - [Academic paper on private security regulation, academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/9296010) [Secondary]
- **So:** the exclusion is a business decision that stands whatever the law says. The advocate is asked only to confirm which law governs private security work and that a guide offering "protection" would be acting unlawfully or outside their registration.

### 1.4 VIP personal data and confidentiality (PDPA 2022)

| Point | What the sources say / proposal | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The law | Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (Act No. 11 of 2022), in force 1 May 2023 (DLA Piper); English text published as GN 395B. "Sensitive personal data" is defined in s. 3 as a list ("includes ..."). Processing sensitive data generally needs prior consent, with limited exceptions. | [TanzLII, GN 395B (PDF)](https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/376041/source_file/b6ac2610a78cbbe3/tz-act-gn-2023-395b-1-publication-document.pdf) [Official, could not read]; [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=TZ) [Secondary]; [FPF overview](https://fpf.org/blog/tanzanias-personal-information-protection-act-overview-key-takeaways-and-context/) [Secondary] |
| What VIP trips involve | Exact location and schedule, hotel and room, passport details, health or diet needs (health is commonly treated as sensitive; **to verify against the s. 3 list**), religion (prayer times, halal), companions, the fact that a well-known person is in the country. | My reading |
| Hosts as recipients | When Kwetuly passes trip details to a host, the host acts for the traveller (or for Kwetuly). Controllers and processors must use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. | [Clyde & Co, obligations](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/02/key-obligations-for-data-controllers-and-processor) [Secondary] |
| Proposals for the advocate | (1) A **confidentiality clause** in the host service terms: no sharing, posting or photographing the traveller, their schedule or location, during or after the trip, without written consent; survives the end of the agreement. (2) No VIP photos or names in Kwetuly marketing, testimonials or "Moments" without separate written consent. (3) Share only what the host needs, as late as possible; delete trip details from chat after a set period. (4) Host applications: collect only what is needed (see business-documents-and-ai.md). (5) No sensitive data in the AI pre-check. (6) Breach procedure. | Proposals, [Unverified] |
| Kwetuly's own registration | Kwetuly's PDPC registration as controller (already an open question in the README) would need to cover this processing. | README |

### 1.5 Wording rules for VIP host profiles

- "VIP" describes the style of service, not a status the guide holds. Avoid "exclusive access", "skip the queue", "private entrance", "guaranteed table", "best seat" unless the provider has confirmed it in writing.
- No "safe", "secure", "protected", "discreet guaranteed".
- Show what Kwetuly checked and when (guide registration number seen on [date]). Commission disclosed.

## 2. "Investment visit host": logistics and introductions only

### 2.1 Proposed scope

Meeting the investor, arranging the visit programme, site-visit logistics (with a licensed driver or operator, since transfers are off), interpreting into Kiswahili (non-legal), introducing local guides, cultural tourism groups and businesses on Kwetuly, and pointing to official TISEZA pages. The /invest page (apps/web/src/app/[lang]/invest/page.tsx, texts in packages/shared/src/invest.ts) already links only to `https://www.tiseza.go.tz` and `https://www.tiseza.go.tz/contacts` and says "This page is not legal or financial advice". That pattern should be kept.

### 2.2 Limits and the law behind them

| Limit | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No investment advice** | The Capital Markets and Securities Act (Cap. 79) defines an "investment adviser" (advising others about securities, or publishing securities analyses as a regular part of business) and requires a CMSA investment adviser's licence (reported ss. 2, 34, 37). This is about **securities**; advice on whether to buy land or build a lodge is not obviously covered, but the safe rule is no recommendation of any investment, share, partner's company or deal. | [CMSA Act, TanzLII](https://www.tanzlii.org/akn/tz/act/1994/5) [Official, not read]; [Licensing Regulations 1996, TanzLII](https://www.tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/1996/417) [Official, not read]; search summaries [Unverified] |
| **No legal advice or legal documents** | Advocates Act (Cap. 341): reported s. 35 (who is qualified), s. 41 "Unqualified person not to act as advocate", s. 42 penalty for pretending to be an advocate, s. 43(1) unqualified persons may not prepare certain instruments for gain (a 2016 High Court ruling read it so). A 2023 Act amended ss. 41-42 (content not seen). So hosts must not draft agreements, land documents or applications for a fee, or explain what the law requires. | [Advocates Act, TanzLII (2019 revision)](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/ord/1954/25/eng@2019-11-30) [Official, not read]; [FB Attorneys Q&A 2013](https://fbattorneys.co.tz/?p=1966) [Secondary]; [High Court ruling 2016, TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/akn/tz/judgment/tzhc/2016/15/eng@2016-11-17/source) [Official, summary only] |
| **No promises of approvals, incentives or speed** | Approvals belong to TISEZA, MNRT, the land and environment authorities. A promise of an outcome or timeline would be misleading (Fair Competition Act, Cap. 285, see README) and invites the "I know someone" model. Banned words: "guaranteed", "fast-track", "VIP processing", "we know the right people", "approval in X days". | My reading; Fair Competition Act in README |
| **No payments to officials, ever** | Prevention and Combating of Corruption Act, 2007 (Act No. 11 of 2007, Cap. 329): s. 15 "Corrupt transactions" (the general offence, used in prosecutions), ss. 16-20 contracts, procurement, auctions, employment; bribery-related provisions reported around s. 23. Amended in 2024 (wider offences, new PCCB powers; effect on s. 15 not checked). PCCB investigates and prosecutes on the mainland. This covers "facilitation" payments, gifts and "fees" to speed a file. Zanzibar has its own anti-corruption law and body (**to verify**: Zanzibar Anti-Corruption and Economic Crimes Authority, ZAECA). | [PCCA 2007, TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/akn/tz/act/2007/11) [Official, contents list seen via search]; [DLA Piper bribery guide](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-th/insights/publications/2019/09/global-bribery-offenses-guide/tanzania) [Secondary]; [Anticor, 2024 amendment](https://anticor.world/en/main/news_page/tanzania_amends_its_anticorruption_legislation) [Secondary] |
| **Official links only** | Only `tiseza.go.tz` and other `.go.tz` pages; no "agents", consultants or paid fast-track services linked by Kwetuly. | /invest page |
| **Who may represent an investor before TISEZA** | **Not found.** The Investment and Special Economic Zones Act, 2025 (Act No. 6 of 2025) created TISEZA (operating from 1 July 2025), replaced the Tanzania Investment Act, 2022, the EPZ Act and the SEZ Act; registration is reported as mandatory for all investors; s. 14 reportedly sets up an integrated electronic system. Nothing found on agents or representatives. **My suggestion:** hosts never file, sign or act "on behalf of" an investor; the investor or their advocate deals with TISEZA. | [The Citizen](https://thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/business/new-authority-set-to-streamline-investment-processes-in-tanzania-5108124) [Secondary]; [Afriwise, Bill analysis](https://www.afriwise.com/blog/analysis-of-the-investment-and-special-economic-zones-bill-2024) [Secondary]; [Chambers 2025](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/investing-in-2025/tanzania) [Secondary] |
| Introductions for a fee | A success fee or commission from a business the host introduces an investor to is a conflict of interest and, if paid by a public body or official, a corruption risk. Proposal: no success fees; the host is paid a disclosed day rate by the investor only. | Proposal |
| Non-citizen activity | GN 487A bars non-citizens from some businesses; an investor's own business plan is for their advocate. The host must not suggest nominee or "front" arrangements (a citizen who assists a non-citizen in a reserved activity is reported to face a fine of TZS 5 million and up to 3 months). | [DLA Piper / IMMMA](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/tanzania/insights/2025/A29574-legal-alert-flyer-opt1-v1) [Secondary] |
| Investor's data | Business plans and identities are confidential; same clause as 1.4. | PDPA 2022 |

## Questions for the advocate

**VIP personal trip host**

1. Does a registered tour guide who accompanies a traveller, plans their days and books restaurants and activities **in the traveller's name** (traveller pays each provider) stay within tour guiding, or is that travel agent / tour operator work needing an MNRT licence?
2. At what point (bundled price, margin on third-party services, taking payment for others) does it become licensed travel agent or tour operator activity?
3. Does the "enlisted under a licensed tour company, TTGA or similar entity" condition (GN 270 of 2015) stop a guide contracting directly with a VIP traveller through Kwetuly?
4. Which law governs private security and close protection work in Tanzania (Cap. 322, police guidelines, other)? Would a guide offering "protection" break that law or their registration? Please confirm Kwetuly's exclusion wording is enough.
5. Which items in the PDPA 2022 s. 3 "sensitive personal data" list are likely in VIP trips (health, religion, others)? What consent and safeguards are needed before Kwetuly passes them to a host?
6. Is the host a processor for Kwetuly, a separate controller, or both? What must the host agreement contain under the PDPA 2022?
7. Is the proposed confidentiality clause (1.4) enforceable against an individual guide, including after the agreement ends? What remedy is realistic?

**Investment visit host**

8. Does introducing investors to local businesses and arranging site visits for a day rate need any licence (CMSA, TISEZA, business licence)?
9. Does the CMSA investment adviser licence reach advice on non-securities investments (land, lodges, joint ventures)? Where exactly is the line for a host?
10. Under the Investment and Special Economic Zones Act, 2025, who may represent or act for an investor before TISEZA (only the investor, an advocate, an authorised agent)? Is any agent registration required?
11. Which acts by a non-advocate host would breach the Advocates Act (ss. 35, 41-43 as reported): filling forms, translating documents, explaining procedures?
12. Please confirm the relevant anti-corruption provisions (PCCA 2007 s. 15 and others, after the 2024 amendments) and the Zanzibar equivalent. Could Kwetuly be liable if a host pays an official, and what policy and controls would you expect?
13. Are success fees or commissions from introduced businesses lawful for a host or for Kwetuly? Should they be banned?
14. Is the draft disclaimer (2.3) adequate under the Fair Competition Act? Please rewrite it.

**Other services**

15. Airport meet-and-greet: does greeting in the public arrivals hall need any TAA, KADCO or Zanzibar Airports Authority permit? Who may offer fast-track services?
16. Zanzibar weddings: please confirm the formalities for foreigners (21-day notice, no waiver, documents, ZCSRA Marriage Return) and whether a wedding host needs any licence or registration as a marriage officiant or planner.
17. Filming: which permits apply (Tanzania Film Board, TANAPA, TCAA for drones, Zanzibar), the current fees and the legal basis? Could a fixer or Kwetuly be liable for a client filming without a permit?
18. Diving: which bodies license dive centres and dive boats on the mainland and in Zanzibar, and which marine park permits apply? What must Kwetuly see before listing a dive operator?
19. Should any of these services wait until Kwetuly's company is registered, and does the future shareholding change the answers?
