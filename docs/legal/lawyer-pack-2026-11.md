The board text "TTB guide licence" was wrong and is replaced. Please confirm: is the TANAPA licence a separate park licence on top of MNRT registration, or does it replace it? Does it cover only mountain and safari guides, and who licenses cultural and city guides? Is there a register Kwetuly can check? (roles Q1-5; guide checks Q31-35)
2. With Kwetuly only listing providers, taking a **disclosed commission** and using a Flutterwave split, does Kwetuly need a tourism licence (tour operator or travel agent) or BoT approval? **Guides and porters**

5. Walter says guides are **independent contractors** who deal with travellers directly. Does the "enlisted with a licensed tour company, TTGA or similar" condition in GN 270 of 2015 allow that? Can Kwetuly's terms safely say so? (travel-agent Q13)
