## 3. What the labels on Kwetuly mean

We try to say exactly what we know, and no more.

- **Guide checks.** A guide profile lists each check Kwetuly has done ("Identity checked", "Guide licence checked", "First aid checked") with the date. It means a Kwetuly team member looked at the document on that date. It does not mean Kwetuly guarantees the guide, their skills or their conduct. A first-aid check disappears when the certificate expires.
- **"Checked by Kwetuly" badge on businesses.** The business paid a yearly fee for a check. The badge lists what was checked and when, and until when the badge runs. It is **not a recommendation**.
- **"Listed".** The business is on Kwetuly but we have not checked it.
- **"Recommended by guides".** Shown only when real guides on Kwetuly recommended the place.
- **Commission note.** Where you see "Kwetuly and guides may earn a commission when you buy here", the business has agreed to pay Kwetuly (and sometimes the guide who referred you) a share of what you spend. The price you pay is [not increased because of the commission: Walter to confirm with each business].
- **"Sample guide" / "Sample listing".** Test data. It is not a real person or business offering services. Enquiries go to Kwetuly, not to anyone named.
- **"Details being re-checked".** Kwetuly has not yet confirmed the trip details. Confirm everything with the provider.
- **Reviews** come only from travellers who really dealt with the guide (an accepted or completed prep call, or a confirmed booking). We do not edit reviews, but we may hide reviews that break section 8.

## 4. Enquiries, messages, trip requests and offers

4.1 **Enquiries and messages.** You can message guides and send enquiries to businesses. To protect you from scams, phone numbers, emails and WhatsApp links are hidden until a booking or call is confirmed. Please keep conversations on Kwetuly until then. Kwetuly staff may read messages to keep people safe, check reports and fix problems.

4.2 **Trip requests.** You can describe the trip you want. Approved guides and business accounts can see it and send offers with a price, itinerary, inclusions and exclusions, and how long the offer is valid.

4.3 **Accepting an offer** is your agreement with that guide or company on the terms in the offer. The other offers are declined, both of you can see each other's email and phone, and a private chat opens. Read the itinerary, what is included and excluded, and the cancellation terms before accepting. Ask the guide or company for anything that is not written in the offer.

4.4 **Prep calls** are calls with a guide at the price the guide sets. [How the call is paid at soft launch: directly to the guide / through Kwetuly: Walter to confirm.]
