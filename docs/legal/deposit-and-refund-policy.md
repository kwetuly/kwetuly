> **Drafting note: the big regulatory question first.** The current design has Rafrika collect deposits through Selcom into Rafrika's merchant account, show guides "Held by Rafrika", and pay guides out by hand from the admin page. Holding customers' money for third parties and paying it on may need Bank of Tanzania approval under the National Payment Systems Act, 2015 and its regulations (Payment Systems (Licensing and Approval) Regulations, 2015; Payment Systems (Electronic Money) Regulations, 2015). It may also make Rafrika look like a travel agent under the Tourism Act, 2008. The advocate should advise on the safest structure before deposits go on, for example:
> - (A) Rafrika as the guide's **commercial agent** that collects on their behalf (need for BoT approval to be checked);
> - (B) **direct split / settlement** by Selcom (or another licensed provider) straight to the guide's wallet, so Rafrika never holds the money;
> - (C) keep deposits **off** and let guides take deposits directly.
> This draft is written for (A) with placeholders, because that is how the code works today. The wording "held by Rafrika" must not be used until the advocate confirms the structure. Rafrika must not offer any stored balance, wallet or credits (parked idea) without BoT approval.

## 1. What a deposit is

1.1 Some guides and companies ask for a deposit to confirm a trip. The deposit percentage is shown in the offer or group trip before you accept or join (for example "Deposit to confirm: 30%").
1.2 The deposit is part of the trip price, not an extra fee. [Rafrika charges no fee to travellers on deposits / Rafrika's fee is [X] and is shown before you pay.]
1.3 Deposits are taken only for: (a) an accepted trip offer, and (b) an accepted spot on a group trip. [Prep calls and packages: not through Rafrika at launch.]
1.4 The rest of the price is paid directly to the guide or company as you agree with them, unless the offer says otherwise.

2.3 You get a receipt by email and in the app when the payment succeeds.

3.1 Rafrika receives the deposit [as the collecting agent of the guide or company / see drafting note].

4.4 **Your join request was declined**, or a payment was taken by mistake or twice: 100% refund.

4.5 **Events outside anyone's control** (for example park closures, natural disasters, government travel bans): [the guide and traveller agree a new date or a refund of [X]% of the deposit; costs already paid to third parties such as park fees may be deducted if shown with receipts].

4.6 **Changes.** If the guide changes the price, dates or main itinerary after you paid, you may cancel and get 100% back.

## 6. Disputes about the trip itself

If the trip took place but was not as described, contact the guide or company first. If you cannot agree, Rafrika will look at the offer, messages and evidence from both sides and may [refund part of a deposit not yet passed on / remove the guide's checks or profile]. Rafrika cannot decide claims about money paid directly to the guide. You keep your rights under Tanzanian consumer law and can complain to the Fair Competition Commission.
