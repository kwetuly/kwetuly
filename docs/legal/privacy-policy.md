## 2. The short version

- We collect what you give us (account, phone, profile, messages, photos and videos, enquiries) and a small amount of usage data.
- Guides and businesses you contact see what you send them. Your phone, email and WhatsApp are hidden in messages until a booking or call is confirmed.
- Group-chat photos and videos are private to the chat. Kwetuly only shows one publicly if the person who shared it says yes, Kwetuly picks it, and nobody in the chat objects. See the separate Group-chat media consent.
- Kwetuly staff can read messages and chats to keep people safe, deal with reports and fix problems.
- We use service providers, some outside Tanzania (listed in section 7).
- We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising trackers.
- You can see, correct, download and delete your data. "Delete my account" is in your account settings.

## 4. Who can see your data

- **Everyone (public)**: approved guide profiles (not private details), approved business listings, approved packages and group trips, reviews with the reviewer's chosen name, and group-chat items that are featured with consent (with the sharer's display name).
- **The guide, business or company you contact**: your enquiry and what you send them. Contact details only after a booking or call is confirmed (for one-to-one messages) or once an offer is accepted (for trip requests, where both sides see each other's email and phone).
- **Group trip organisers**: your name, contact, number of seats and message when you ask to join. Accepted members can see the organiser's contact.
- **Other chat members**: your display name, messages and media in that chat. Anyone you give an invite link to can join a chat after signing in.
- **Guides who match a trip request**: open trip requests, including your first name as you gave it and trip details, are visible to approved guides and business accounts so they can send offers.
- **Kwetuly admins**: everything needed to run and protect the service, including private documents, message originals, chats and reports. Admins are [NUMBER] named people bound by confidentiality. [Describe the access log, if any.]
- **Service providers** acting for us (section 7).
- **Authorities**, only when the law requires it, or to protect someone's life or safety. We will tell you unless the law forbids it.
- **A buyer or successor** of Kwetuly's business, under the same promises in this policy.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## 5. Contact details in messages
To protect travellers and guides from scams, Kwetuly automatically hides phone numbers, emails and WhatsApp links in one-to-one messages until a booking or call is confirmed, or an admin unlocks the conversation. The original text is kept so admins can deal with reports.

## 9. Security
