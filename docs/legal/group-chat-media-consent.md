## 1. How group chats work

1.1 A group chat is for people travelling together. Every approved group trip has one with the organiser and the accepted travellers. Accepting a trip offer opens one for the traveller and the guide or company. Anyone can also start a chat and invite people with a link.
1.2 Anyone with an invite link can join a chat after signing in (except trip chats, which are only for people on the trip). Share invite links only with people you want in the chat. The chat owner can remove members.
1.3 You can share messages, photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC) and short videos (MP4, MOV, WebM, up to 50 MB).
1.4 **Who can see what you share:** the members of that chat, and Kwetuly admins (to keep people safe, deal with reports and fix problems). Nobody else, unless the item is shared publicly as in section 2.
1.5 Members can download and share items to other apps. Kwetuly cannot control what members do with items they download.

## 2. Public sharing: three steps, all needed

Kwetuly may show a photo, video or tip from a chat on the public site (for example on "Traveller moments") **only while all three are true**:

1. **The person who shared it says yes** by turning on "Kwetuly may use this publicly".
2. **Kwetuly chooses it** (most items are never chosen).
3. **Nobody in the chat objects.** Any member can press "I'm in this: please don't share". While any objection stands, Kwetuly will not show the item.

If the sharer presses "Stop sharing", or anyone objects, the item comes off the site straight away. [Copies already shared on Kwetuly's social media: see 4.4.]

## 3. What you confirm when you say "Kwetuly may use this publicly"

When you turn this on, you confirm that:
- (a) you took the photo or video yourself, or you have the right to share it;
- (b) **every person who can be recognised in it agrees** to it being shown publicly by Kwetuly, including people who are not in the chat;
- (c) it does not show a child (under 18) who can be recognised, unless a parent or guardian agrees [advocate: is this enough, or should children be excluded entirely?];
- (d) it does not show anything private, unsafe or illegal (for example nudity, someone's ID or bank card, a location that puts someone at risk, or restricted sites where filming is not allowed);
- (e) the caption or tip is your own and does not contain personal data about others.

The objection system only protects people who are members of the chat; people who are not members (local residents, porters, other tourists, children) can only be protected by the sharer's confirmation and Kwetuly's own review. ## 4. What Kwetuly may do with an item you share publicly

4.1 You keep ownership of your photo or video.
4.2 You give Kwetuly a free, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to show the item, with the display name you use on Kwetuly, on Kwetuly's website and app [and on Kwetuly's own social media accounts], while your consent stands. Kwetuly may resize or crop it, but will not edit it in a misleading way.
4.3 Kwetuly will show your display name as "Shared by [name]". [Option for anonymity: Walter to decide.]
4.4 [Social media and other marketing: Kwetuly will only post an item outside the Kwetuly site if you have given separate permission for that item / If you stop sharing, Kwetuly will remove the item from its website and app straight away and delete its own social media posts of it within [7] days, but cannot recall copies others have made.] Walter to choose.
4.5 Kwetuly does not sell your photos or videos and does not use them in paid advertising [without asking you again].
4.6 Kwetuly does not pay for items shared.

## 5. Objecting and changing your mind

5.1 **The sharer** can press "Stop sharing" at any time. The item leaves the public site straight away.
5.2 **Any member of the chat** can object with "I'm in this: please don't share". You do not have to give a reason. You can withdraw your objection with "Allow sharing again".
5.3 **People who are not in the chat**, or who left it, can ask Kwetuly to remove an item at [CONTACT EMAIL / report form]. Kwetuly will take it down within [48 hours] while it looks into the request, and will not show it again if they are recognisable in it.
5.4 Deleting a message or your account removes the item from the chat and from the public site.

## 6. Kwetuly's review

Before featuring an item, a Kwetuly team member checks it against section 3 and Kwetuly's content rules. Kwetuly may refuse or remove any item without giving a reason.

## 7. Storage and security

Chat photos and videos are stored in private storage that only chat members and Kwetuly admins can open. Featured items are readable by anyone while they are featured. See the Privacy Policy for where data is stored and for how long.

## Open questions for the advocate

1. Under the PDPA, is an image of a recognisable person their personal data, and is the sharer's confirmation that "everyone in it agrees" a valid basis for Kwetuly to publish it? Or must Kwetuly collect consent from each recognisable person directly?
2. Is the opt-out model for other chat members (they can object) acceptable, or should public use need opt-in from every member who appears?
3. Children: should Kwetuly refuse any public item with a recognisable child, even with parental consent?
4. Do the "Traveller moments" pages make Kwetuly an online content service provider needing a TCRA licence under the Electronic and Postal Communications (Online Content) Regulations, 2020 (amended 2022)? What takedown duties apply?
5. Copyright: is the licence in section 4 enough under the Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act for Kwetuly's use, including on social media?
6. Filming restrictions: are there places in Tanzania (for example government, military or certain park areas) where photos must not be published, which we should name?
7. Is "Kwetuly admins can read all chats" acceptable for private group chats, or should admin access be limited to reported content?
