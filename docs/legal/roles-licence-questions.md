## 3. Questions for the advocate

### A. Guide registration (all guide roles)

1. Which body registers or licenses tour guides today on the mainland: MNRT (Director of Tourism under GN 270 of 2015), the Tanzania Tourist Board (TTB), or another? Does TTB have any role in guide licences? (S3; no TTB source found.)
2. What is the parent Act and its current citation: Tourism Act No. 29 of 2008, "No. 11 of 2008", or "Tourism Act, Cap. 65" as a 2026 news report cites it? Is GN 270 of 2015 still in force, and has it been amended? (S2, S4)
3. Does the registration certificate or identity card name a **category** (mountain, safari, cultural, city, birding, marine)? May a guide registered for one category guide in another? If categories exist, may Kwetuly show them as "stated by the guide" until checked?
4. What exactly does "enlisted under a licensed tour company, TTGA or similar established entity" require? May a registered guide sell services directly to travellers on his own account? (Same as travel-agent-and-transfers.md Q13; please answer once for all roles.)
5. How long is registration valid, and is there a renewal date Kwetuly can record as "valid until"? (Same as advocate-questions-guide-checks-2026-11.md Q35.)
6. Is the TATO driver-guide code of conduct (May 2025) binding on anyone, or voluntary? May Kwetuly mention it without TATO's permission? (S17)

### B. Mountain guide and Kilimanjaro (KINAPA, TANAPA)

7. Must every climb of Kilimanjaro or Meru be booked through a company holding the MNRT mountain climbing licence? May a registered mountain guide take a traveller up on his own, without a licensed company? Under which rule? (S1, S4)
8. Which KINAPA and TANAPA rules apply to guides, assistant guides, porters and cooks in the park (registration, guide-to-climber ratios, first aid or WFR, load limits, pay timing)? Where is the gazetted or published text? (S10; no official text found.)
9. The Ministry said in August 2026 that KRTO must not act as a regulator or certifier (S4). May Kwetuly still show an operator's KPAP partner status as a fact it saw, worded "listed by KPAP on [date]", or does that risk suggesting an official status?
