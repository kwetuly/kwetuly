### 1.3 Fees

**I could not find the current fee.** In short:
- Fees are set by the **Tourism (Fees and Charges) Regulations, 2015 (GN 267 of 2015)**, with annual fees by class in the Second Schedule. TanzLII flags amendments that have not yet been applied. ([TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/2015/267)) [Official, schedule not read]
- History, all [Secondary]: a flat USD 5,000 (foreign-owned) or USD 2,000 (local) before a 2018 restructuring by fleet size ([The EastAfrican](https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business/tanzania-announces-new-licence-fees-for-tour-operators-1381654); [TanzaniaInvest, 2018](https://tanzaniainvest.com/tourism/new-tourism-license-fee-structure-2018)). The 2021/22 budget **proposed** cutting the fee for travel agents and tour operators from about TZS 4.6 million to TZS 1.1 million ([The Citizen](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-publishes-new-fees-for-tourism-players-2619548)). The 2025 cuts reportedly targeted mountain-climbing agencies and guide fees ([ATTA, May 2025](https://atta.travel/resource/tanzania-cuts-tourism-licence-fees-to-stimulate-local-investment-and-conservation.html); [Tourism Update](https://www.tourismupdate.com/article/tanzania-reduces-tourism-licence-fee)).
- **No fee should appear in any Kwetuly text until the advocate confirms it.**

### 1.4 What "travel agent profile" can mean on Kwetuly

A **traveller** adding the profile is quite likely to be a foreigner, so there are two very different cases:

| Case | Example | Likely position [Unverified] |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (i) Agency **based in Tanzania** | A Dar es Salaam or Arusha agency books flights, hotels and tours | Needs the MNRT travel agent licence. Only 100% Tanzanian-owned companies can hold it (section 1.2). |
| (ii) Agency **based abroad** | A German agency sends clients and uses Kwetuly to find a local guide | Probably regulated in its own country, not by TALA, **provided** it buys ground services from Tanzanian licence holders and does not itself operate in Tanzania. Whether it can contract directly with a registered guide, rather than a licensed tour operator, is an open question (see section 2.5, guide enlistment). |
| (iii) Individual "agent" with no company | A traveller who wants to resell guide trips to friends for a commission | Probably cannot hold a Tanzanian travel agent licence. If they sell Tanzanian tourism services from inside Tanzania, this may be unlicensed activity. **Suggestion: do not offer a travel agent profile to individuals.** |

### 1.5 Draft approval checklist for a travel agent profile (for partnerships and operations-manager)

Record **what** was seen and **when**, never "recommended" or "verified travel agent" in general terms.

1. **Where the business is based.** Tanzania mainland, Zanzibar, or abroad? Pick the checklist below.
2. **Tanzania-based:**
   - BRELA certificate of incorporation, or business name registration. The name must match the profile. Check on [BRELA](https://www.brela.go.tz/).
   - MNRT tourism licence of the **right class** (travel agent, or tour operator if it runs ground tours): licence number, holder name, class and expiry date. Re-check every year at renewal.
   - TIN certificate. Local government business licence if the advocate says one is needed as well.
   - **No profile if the licence has expired.** Set a reminder before the expiry date.
   - Kwetuly cannot check the shareholders' nationality itself. It relies on the licence: only Tanzanian-owned companies are reported to receive one.
3. **Abroad-based:**
   - Company registration in its home country and, where that country has one, its travel-trade licence or bond. For example, a package-travel insolvency protection certificate in EU countries [Unverified, not researched].
   - Its written promise that in Tanzania it uses only licensed operators and registered guides.
   - The profile says "Based in [country]. Not licensed in Tanzania. Uses local guides through Kwetuly."
4. **Contact person** with authority to act for the business, and ID. Personal data is handled under the PDPA 2022: collect only what is needed and keep it only as long as needed.
5. **Profile wording.** For example: "Tourism licence no. … (travel agent) seen by Kwetuly on [date], valid until [date]." Never "licensed and trusted" or "recommended".
6. **Commission disclosure.** If the agent adds a margin or earns a commission, the traveller must see it (Kwetuly rule).
7. **No resale of Kwetuly's own fees or deposits**, and no stored credit (see the payments questions in lessons-payments-items-research.md).

### 2.1 Road transport: LATRA

**My reading [Unverified]:** a guide who drives travellers for a fee in **their own privately registered car** is probably carrying passengers "for hire or reward". The car then needs a LATRA licence (private hire or tour service, depending on the category that now applies), and the guide needs a LATRA driver registration and, for tour vehicles, the new tourism certification. It may also need commercial registration, inspection, and possibly a tracking device. A tracking device is reported for buses; I don't know whether it applies to small vehicles. **I found nothing that exempts a single guide with one car.**

### 2.2 Driving licence class

- Classes C, C1, C2 and C3 are commonly treated as the classes for **public service (passenger) vehicles**. Uber's Tanzania driver requirements ask for class C, C1, C2 or C3. ([Uber Tanzania requirements](https://www.uber.com/en-TZ/drive/requirements/) [Secondary: a company's own page, not law]; forum and job-advert sources [Unverified])
- Holders of classes C, C1, C2, C3 and E are reported to need refresher training at every renewal under the **Road Traffic (Examination and Re-testing of Drivers) Regulations, 2015** ([FB Attorneys](https://fbattorneys.co.tz/?p=13472)) [Secondary].
- **I could not find an official table** showing which class covers which vehicle, for example a private car (class D?) versus a paid passenger car or minibus. The Road Traffic Act (Cap. 168) and its regulations need checking. [Unverified]
- A **foreign** driving licence or an international driving permit is very unlikely to be enough for commercial driving, and only Tanzanian citizens may guide anyway (GN 487A). [Unverified]

### 2.3 Insurance (Kwetuly checks it but does not sell or arrange it)

- **Motor Vehicles Insurance Act (Cap. 169)**: third-party insurance is compulsory, and a "policy of insurance" includes a cover note. ([TanzLII copy, 2002 revision](https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/316255/source_file/0cf5e5e8244f8664/1945-18.pdf)) [Official, old consolidated text; current amendments not checked]
- **Whether the compulsory cover protects fare-paying passengers**, and whether a **commercial or PSV policy** with passenger legal liability is required for a licensed passenger vehicle, I could not confirm. Insurers' schedules show passenger liability as a separate cover ([CRDB motor limits schedule](https://crdbbank.co.tz/storage/app/media/Forms%20and%20Guides/Terms%20and%20Conditions/schedule-of-motor-limits-0034.pdf) [Secondary, commercial]). A **private-use policy may be void, or the insurer may refuse to pay, if the car is used for hire.** [Unverified, my reading]
- **Checking a sticker:** TIRA's MIS system reportedly lets anyone check a sticker and cover note online, or by SMS to 15200. The portal address given by a blog is `tiramis.tira.go.tz`. ([The Citizen](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/system-launched-to-check-illegally-insuared-vehicles-2591970) [Secondary]; portal address [Unverified]). Display rules: **Display of Road Licence and Evidence of Motor Vehicle Insurance Regulations, 1979** ([TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/1979/95/eng@2002-07-31)) [Official, not read].
- **Kwetuly rule:** Kwetuly does not sell, bundle or recommend any insurance (NIC/TIRA agency approval would be needed). It only asks to see the guide's own policy, and links only to official sites.

### 2.4 Is a TALA (tourism) licence also needed for transfers?

Probably yes in some form, but **this is the main open question.** [Unverified]
- MNRT's Class A list includes **car hire/rental** and **tour operator** ("professional ground transport for game viewing"). Both involve vehicles carrying tourists ([TISEZA](https://procedures.tiseza.go.tz/objective/247?l=en)) [Official].
- Car hire is reported to need a **fleet of at least three roadworthy vehicles, rising to five or more within 24 months**, and to be open only to fully Tanzanian-owned **companies** ([Rive & Co](https://www.rive.co.tz/your-guide-to-registering-and-licensing-a-tourism-business-in-tanzania/)) [Secondary]. Tour operators are reported to need vehicles registered in the company's name ([GN 213 of 1998 summary](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/1998/213/eng@2002-07-31)) [Unverified].
- **So an individual guide with one car may not qualify for any MNRT licence class at all.** If carrying tourists for reward counts as a licensable "tourism activity", a freelance guide doing transfers on their own account could be operating without a licence. Lawful routes might then be:
  1. The guide drives **for** a licensed tour operator or car hire company, which holds the TALA and LATRA licences for the vehicle, and the transfer is sold under that company's name.
  2. A LATRA licence alone is enough for a plain point-to-point transfer, as for a taxi or private hire, because it is transport and not a "tour". **I found no source for this.**
- **Tour guide registration (s. 42 of the Act; Tourism (Tour Guides) Regulations, 2015, GN 270 of 2015)** covers guiding, not driving. One registration condition is reported to be that the guide is "enlisted under a licensed tour company, Tanzania Tour Guide Association or similar established entity". Registration is reportedly open only to Tanzanian citizens aged 21+, with secondary education, a valid first-aid certificate and knowledge of the area. A guide operating unregistered faces a fine of at least TZS 1 million, up to 6 months' prison, or both. ([GN 270 of 2015, TanzLII](https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/2015/270/eng@2015-07-17) [Official, wording from a search summary]; [FB Attorneys](https://fbattorneys.co.tz/registration-of-tour-guides/) [Secondary]; [Rive & Co](https://www.rive.co.tz/your-guide-to-registering-and-licensing-a-tourism-business-in-tanzania/) [Secondary])

### 2.6 Draft approval checklist for a transfer package (for operations-manager and guide-success)

Approve a transfer package only if **all** of these have been seen, each with a recorded date, and with a reminder before any expiry date:

1. **Tour guide registration** (Tanzanian citizen) is already on the guide profile.
2. **Who sells the transfer.** Either:
   - (a) the guide drives for a **licensed company**: the company's MNRT licence (tour operator or car hire), number, class and expiry, and a letter from the company that the guide drives for it and that this vehicle is covered; or
   - (b) the guide's own route, **only if the advocate confirms one exists** (section 2.4). Until then, (b) is not offered.
3. **Vehicle:**
   - Registration card. The plate, make and seats must match the package.
   - **LATRA licence** for this vehicle, of the category the advocate names (private hire or tour service), with its number and expiry.
   - Inspection report if LATRA requires one.
   - "Tour Services" marking if the 2025 rules are in force.
4. **Driver:**
   - Tanzanian driving licence of the right class (the advocate to confirm, probably C, C1, C2 or C3).
   - **LATRA driver registration card**, and the tour-driver certificate once it exists.
   - First-aid certificate (already part of guide registration).
5. **Insurance:**
   - Sticker and cover note **for commercial or hire use, with passenger liability**, checked on TIRA MIS, with its expiry date.
   - Kwetuly does not suggest or sell any insurer.
6. **Package rules** (matching docs/tech/one-account-roles-and-sign-in.md):
   - Price per vehicle, in TZS.
   - Maximum passengers equal to the licensed seats.
   - Luggage limit.
   - Pick-up and drop-off places. No park crossings unless separately approved.
   - Waiting time and the flight-delay rule.
   - Cancellation.
   - Commission disclosed.
7. **Profile wording.** For example: "Vehicle T 123 ABC: LATRA licence no. … and insurance valid to [date], seen by Kwetuly on [date]." Never "safe", "fully insured" or "recommended".
8. **Re-check** every year, and whenever the vehicle or driver changes. Suspend the package automatically when any recorded document expires.

## 3. Risks to Kwetuly from listing them

| Risk | Why | How to reduce it (for the advocate to confirm) |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Questions for the advocate

**Travel agents and Kwetuly's own position**

1. Which law governs tourism licences today: the Tourism Act, 2008 (No. 29 or No. 11?), the Tourist Agents (Licensing) Act and its 1998 regulations, or both? What is the correct name of the licence and of the issuing body (TALA, TTLB, TTBL)?
2. Please confirm that a travel agent licence is available only to companies 100% owned by Tanzanian citizens, and give the legal source (s. 58(2) of the Tourism Act? GN 487A of 2025? a regulation?). Is the rule that two staff must hold IATA qualifications correct?
3. Can an **individual** or sole proprietor hold a travel agent or tour operator licence, or only a company?
4. What are the current annual fees for travel agent, tour operator and car hire licences, after the amendments to the Tourism (Fees and Charges) Regulations, 2015? Do licences expire on 31 December or 12 months after issue?
5. Can a licence be checked in a public register or online? If not, how should Kwetuly confirm that a licence certificate is genuine and current?
6. Does a travel agency **based abroad** need any Tanzanian licence to send clients to, or contract directly with, a registered Tanzanian guide booked through Kwetuly? Must it go through a licensed Tanzanian tour operator?
7. Does Kwetuly need a tourism licence itself if it (a) only lists providers and receives a disclosed commission, (b) collects payments as in options A to D of lessons-payments-items-research.md, or (c) packages "Signature trips"? If any non-citizen holds shares in the future Kwetuly company, which of these would be closed to it?
8. Is the draft travel agent checklist (section 1.5) enough? What should be added or removed?

**Transfers**

9. Does a registered tour guide who drives travellers for a fee in their **own** car need (a) a LATRA licence, and which category (private hire, tour service, or other), (b) commercial registration of the vehicle, (c) an inspection report or tracking device?
10. Are the 2025 LATRA tour-vehicle rules ("Tour Services" marking, tourists only, driver exams and certification) gazetted and in force? Under which GN, and what are the current fees? Did the July 2026 amendments change them?
11. Which driving licence class is required to carry paying passengers in a saloon car, an SUV and a minibus? Under which instrument?
12. Is a TALA/MNRT licence (car hire or tour operator) needed for a plain point-to-point transfer, such as airport to hotel? If so, can a one-car individual guide ever qualify, or must the guide drive for a licensed company?
13. Does the Tour Guides Regulations' "enlisted under a licensed tour company, TTGA or similar entity" condition mean a guide may not sell services, including transfers, directly to travellers on their own account?
14. What insurance must a vehicle carrying paying passengers hold under the Motor Vehicles Insurance Act (Cap. 169) and LATRA's rules: third-party only, or passenger liability or PSV cover? Is a private-use policy invalid for hire? Is TIRA MIS the right way for Kwetuly to check a policy?
15. Do airports (Tanzania Airports Authority, KADCO) or national parks (TANAPA, NCAA) require extra permits for transfer vehicles picking up at terminals or crossing park land?
16. Is the draft transfer checklist (section 2.6) enough for Kwetuly to list transfers lawfully? Can Kwetuly rely on documents it has seen, or does it need to check with LATRA, MNRT or TIRA directly?

**Risk and wording**

17. Could Kwetuly be liable for aiding an offence (Tourism Act s. 46, LATRA rules, GN 487A) if a listed guide or agent turns out to be unlicensed despite the checks?
18. How far can the terms limit Kwetuly's liability for road accidents during a listed transfer under Tanzanian law, including the Fair Competition Act and the Standard Form (Consumer Contracts) Regulations, 2014? Should Kwetuly hold its own public liability cover?
19. Is the proposed profile wording ("licence no. … seen by Kwetuly on [date], valid until [date]") safe under the Fair Competition Act? What wording would you change?
20. Under the PDPA 2022, may Kwetuly keep copies of driving licences, NIDA numbers and insurance papers, or only the numbers and expiry dates? For how long after a guide leaves?
21. Do Zanzibar-based guides or agents need a separate Zanzibar tourism licence or transport permit, and which bodies issue them?
