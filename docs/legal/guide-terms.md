## 1. Your relationship with Kwetuly

1.1 You are an independent guide. You are not Kwetuly's employee, agent or partner. You decide which trips you take, your prices and how you work.
1.2 When a traveller agrees a trip, call or package with you, the contract is between you and the traveller. You are responsible for delivering it safely and as described.
1.3 Kwetuly gives you a profile, ways for travellers to find and message you, trip requests to make offers on, group trips, the Guide Hub and jobs board, and (when on) deposit collection.

> Drafting note: confirm that the relationship cannot be treated as employment under the Employment and Labour Relations Act, given Kwetuly's approval and review powers.

## 2. Joining and the checks Kwetuly does

2.1 You apply with a profile. Kwetuly reviews it before it is public. Kwetuly may refuse an application.
2.2 You must hold every licence, registration and permit your work needs, including [the tourist guide licence/registration required by the Tourism Act, 2008 and park-specific requirements, for example for Kilimanjaro National Park], and keep them valid.
2.3 You may upload your ID, guide licence or registration card and first-aid certificate. These stay private: only you and Kwetuly admins can open them.
2.4 Kwetuly shows each check separately with its date: "Identity checked", "Guide licence checked", "First aid checked". A check means a Kwetuly team member looked at the document. It is not a guarantee of your skills. A first-aid check disappears when the certificate expires. If you upload a new document, the related check is cleared until Kwetuly looks again.
2.5 You must tell Kwetuly within [7] days if a licence or certificate is suspended, withdrawn or expires, or if you are charged with an offence linked to your work.
2.6 You must not say or suggest that Kwetuly recommends or guarantees you, or that you were checked for something Kwetuly did not check.

## 3. Your profile and content

3.1 Everything on your profile must be true and up to date: experience, summits, languages, certifications, association, vehicle, prices.
3.2 Use your own photos, or photos you have the right to use. Do not post photos of travellers publicly without their permission.
3.3 Packages are reviewed by Kwetuly before they show, and go back for review when you change them.
3.4 You keep ownership of your content and give Kwetuly a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to show and promote it on Kwetuly and in Kwetuly's marketing [including social media], while your profile is active [and for [PERIOD] after, for marketing already published].
3.5 Profile visibility: public, or "link only" (people need your link).

## 4. Reviews

4.1 Only travellers who really dealt with you can review you. Your reviews stay attached to your Kwetuly profile while you are on Kwetuly.
4.2 You must not write reviews of yourself, ask friends to review you, pay or reward reviews, or pressure travellers.
4.3 You can reply to or report a review. Kwetuly may hide reviews that break the rules; it does not remove honest negative reviews.

> Drafting note: the site says "Your reviews should follow you, not your employer. Every trip you lead on Kwetuly builds your own name, for life." "For life" is a promise Kwetuly cannot keep (accounts can be closed; Kwetuly could stop operating). Suggest "builds your own name on Kwetuly".

## 5. Messages, offers and contact details

5.1 Keep conversations with travellers on Kwetuly until a booking or call is confirmed. Phone numbers, emails and WhatsApp links in messages are hidden until then, and the original text is kept for Kwetuly admins.
5.2 **Offers.** Your offer on a trip request (price, deposit percentage, itinerary, inclusions, exclusions, validity) is a firm offer. If the traveller accepts it, you must deliver that trip at that price, unless you both agree changes in writing.
5.3 You must not take travellers off Kwetuly to avoid deposits or rules, ask for payment to personal accounts that pretend to be Kwetuly, or contact travellers' details for anything other than their trip.
5.4 Treat travellers' personal data with care and only for their trip. Do not share it, sell it, or add travellers to marketing lists without their consent. You may be responsible yourself under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 for how you use it.

## 6. Group trips, prep calls, the Guide Hub and jobs

6.1 Approved guides can run group trips. Kwetuly reviews each trip before it is public and again after important changes. You decide who joins. Honour the price and inclusions you published.
6.2 Prep calls: you set the price and length. Turn up on time, or tell the traveller in advance.
6.3 The Guide Hub (forum, Field news) and jobs board are for verified guides and companies. Be respectful. Do not post travellers' personal data.
6.4 Client handovers and jobs between companies and guides are agreements between them. Kwetuly reviews posts but is not a party and does not guarantee payment.

7.2 **At soft launch, Kwetuly does not collect money for trips.** Travellers pay you directly.

The Deposit and Refund Policy sets out when Kwetuly passes it to you and when it is refunded. [Kwetuly's fee on deposits: NONE / [X]% — Walter to decide; must be disclosed to guides and travellers.]

When a traveller uses it at a business that has a commission agreement with Kwetuly, you may earn the guide share set in that business's agreement, after the business has paid Kwetuly. You must tell travellers that you may earn a commission, and never pressure them to buy or take them only to businesses that pay you. Kwetuly shows travellers a commission note on those businesses.
7.5 You are responsible for your own taxes (including income tax with TRA) on what you earn. Kwetuly may withhold tax where the law requires and will give you a statement.

## 8. Safety and conduct

8.1 Follow the law, park rules and your profession's safety standards. Carry the first-aid equipment and permits your trips need.
8.2 Never be under the influence of alcohol or drugs while guiding. No harassment, discrimination or sexual contact with travellers in your care.
8.3 Report any serious incident (injury, evacuation, death, crime, harassment) involving a Kwetuly traveller to Kwetuly within [24 hours] at [EMERGENCY CONTACT], in addition to the authorities.
8.4 [Insurance: Kwetuly recommends you hold [public liability / professional] insurance. Kwetuly does not sell or arrange insurance.]

## 9. Suspension and ending

9.1 You can close your profile and delete your account at any time. Group trips and accepted bookings you have already agreed still need to be honoured or properly cancelled.
9.2 Kwetuly may hide a profile, package or trip, remove a check, or close an account if you break these terms, if a licence lapses, after serious complaints, or to protect travellers. Kwetuly will tell you why and give you a chance to respond, unless there is an urgent safety risk.

## 10. Responsibility

10.1 You are responsible for your services and for any claim by a traveller about them. You agree to cover Kwetuly's reasonable costs if a claim against Kwetuly is caused by your breach of these terms or your negligence [advocate to confirm scope].
10.2 Kwetuly provides the platform as it is, with reasonable care. Kwetuly's total responsibility to you is limited to [AMOUNT / fees you paid to Kwetuly in the last 12 months], except where the law does not allow a limit.
