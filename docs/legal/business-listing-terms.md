# Part A. Listing

A1. **Who can list.** Hotels, lodges, homestays, hostels, Airbnb-style apartments and other rentals, curio shops, gem shops, gear rental, transport and car hire, restaurants, spas, salons, nightlife and similar businesses in Tanzania.

A2. **Listing is free.** Rafrika reviews each listing before it is public and may refuse or remove a listing.

A3. **Your licences.** You confirm that your business holds every licence, registration and permit it needs (for example a business licence, and any tourism licence required for accommodation, transport or car hire under the Tourism Act, 2008), and that you will keep them valid. Rafrika does not check this unless you buy the badge (Part B), and a listing without the badge shows "Listed: we have not checked it".

A4. **Accurate information.** Your name, type, location, photos, prices and description must be true and current. Photos must show your real place or products, and you must have the right to use them.

A5. **Private details.** Your WhatsApp number, licence document and Rafrika's payment note are kept private: only you and Rafrika admins can see them.

A6. **Enquiries and order requests.** Travellers can send you enquiries. Curio, gem and gear-rental shops can receive order requests (delivery to a hotel, or held for collection). You must confirm the price and how to pay before sending anything. **Travellers pay you directly; Rafrika does not take payment for your goods or services.** You are responsible for the goods, services, receipts, delivery, refunds and any legal documents (for example gemstone certificates or export papers).

A7. **Travellers' data.** Use travellers' contact details only to answer them and serve them. Do not add them to marketing lists without their consent, share or sell them. You are responsible for your own compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

A8. **Conduct.** No fake reviews, misleading prices, bait-and-switch, harassment or discrimination. Do not say or suggest that Rafrika recommends you.

A9. **Licence to Rafrika.** You keep ownership of your content and allow Rafrika to show it on Rafrika and in Rafrika's marketing while your listing is active.

A10. **Ending.** You can remove your listing at any time. Rafrika may hide or remove it if you break these terms, after serious complaints, or if information turns out to be false. Rafrika will tell you why unless there is an urgent safety reason.

A11. **Sample listings.** Before launch, Rafrika showed some sample listings built from public information, marked "Sample listing". They are removed before public launch. If your business appeared as a sample and you want it changed or removed, contact [CONTACT].

# Part B. The "Checked by Rafrika" badge

B1. **What it is.** A yearly, paid check of your business by Rafrika. The badge shows on your listing **with the list of what Rafrika checked and the date**, and the date it ends.

B2. **What it is not.** The badge is **not a recommendation**, not a rating, and not a guarantee of quality, safety or service. Rafrika will always show travellers this explanation next to the badge: "Businesses pay a yearly fee for this check. It shows what we looked at. It is not a recommendation." You must not describe the badge as a recommendation, award or certification in your own marketing. "Recommended by guides" is a separate label that only comes from real guide recommendations and cannot be bought.

B3. **What Rafrika checks.** Rafrika checks only the items listed below that apply, and shows only the ones that passed:
- (a) your business licence (Rafrika looks at the document and that it is in date; Rafrika does not confirm it with the issuing authority unless stated);
- (b) the owner's or manager's identity;
- (c) that your photos match the place, by video call or a visit (the badge says which);
- [(d) any other check Walter approves; each check must be described in plain words].

> Drafting note: the business page currently promises "We check your business licence, the owner's identity and that your photos match the place, by video call or a visit." The badge display must say "video call" or "visit" for each badge, because they are different strengths of check. The admin stores `badge_checks`, `badge_checked_at` and `badge_valid_until`.

B4. **Fee.** The yearly fee is [AMOUNT, currently "ask Rafrika": Walter to set] [including / excluding VAT]. Rafrika will contact you on WhatsApp to arrange the check and the payment and will give you an invoice/receipt. The fee is the same for every business of the same type [Walter to confirm] and does not depend on passing.

B5. **If a check does not pass.** If one item cannot be confirmed, Rafrika shows only the items that passed. If no item passes, there is no badge and [Rafrika refunds [X]% of the fee / the fee is not refunded because the check was done: Walter and advocate to decide]. Rafrika will tell you what was missing so you can ask for a new check.

B6. **How long it lasts.** One year from the check date. After that, the badge disappears unless you renew and pass a new check. No automatic renewal or charge.

B7. **Changes during the year.** Tell Rafrika within [14] days if your licence lapses, the owner changes, or the place changes significantly. Rafrika may remove the badge early (without refund for the remaining time [advocate to confirm fairness]) if information was false, the licence lapses, or there are serious, credible complaints about the items checked. Rafrika will tell you why.

B8. **No influence on ranking.** Having the badge [does / does not] change where your listing appears. [Walter to decide; if it does, travellers must be told, e.g. "Checked businesses are shown first".]

B9. **Your information.** Documents you give for the check stay private (owner and admins only) and are kept as set out in the Privacy Policy.

# Part C. Referral commissions

C1. **Separate written agreement.** Commissions only apply if you and Rafrika sign a referral commission agreement (Schedule 1). Each business has its own agreement.

C2. **How it works.** Approved guides have a personal referral code (for example "RFxxxxxx", also as a QR code). When a traveller shows a code and buys from you, you record the sale in your Rafrika dashboard (date, amount in TZS, code, optional note). Rafrika invoices you monthly for the agreed share. The guide's share comes out of Rafrika's total share [or is paid by you separately: to confirm].

C3. **Disclosure to travellers.** While the agreement is active, your listing shows: "Rafrika and guides may earn a commission when you buy here." You agree to this note. You must not hide it or tell travellers there is no commission.

C4. **No higher prices for referred travellers.** You must not charge travellers who use a code more than you would charge them without one. [Walter to confirm this is the rule.]

C5. **Honest reporting.** You must report every referred sale honestly and on time, keep your sales records for [PERIOD], and let Rafrika check them on reasonable notice [advocate: is an audit right appropriate for small shops?].

C6. **No pressure.** Neither you nor the guide may pressure travellers to buy. Rafrika may end the agreement if travellers complain of pressure or misleading sales.

C7. **Tax.** Each party handles its own taxes. Rafrika's invoices will show [VAT if registered]. [Withholding tax treatment: advocate/accountant to confirm.]

## Open questions for the advocate

1. Is a paid "Checked by Rafrika" badge, with the explanation shown, compliant with the misleading-conduct rules in the Fair Competition Act 2003? Is "Checked by Rafrika" itself a safe name, or should it be "Documents checked by Rafrika"?
2. Can the fee be non-refundable if no item passes (B5)? Can Rafrika remove a badge early without a refund (B7)?
3. If the badge affects ranking, what disclosure is required?
4. Referral commissions: are commissions from shops/hotels to a platform and to guides lawful in Tanzania for all business types listed (including gem dealers and accommodation)? Any rules on commissions paid to licensed tourist guides?
5. Does collecting commissions on hotel or tour sales make Rafrika a "travel agent" requiring a tourism licence?
6. Tax: VAT on badge fees and commissions; withholding tax on commission shares paid to guides; EFD receipt duties.
7. Are these business terms (B2B) outside the FCC Standard Form Consumer Contract regime (secondary sources say B2B standard contracts are excluded), and do sole-trader homestay owners count as consumers?
8. Sample listings that used real businesses' public information during testing: any liability, and what should we tell those businesses?
9. Audit right in C5: appropriate for small businesses?
