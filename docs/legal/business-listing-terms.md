# Part A. Listing

A1. **Who can list.** Hotels, lodges, homestays, hostels, Airbnb-style apartments and other rentals, curio shops, gem shops, gear rental, transport and car hire, restaurants, spas, salons, nightlife and similar businesses in Tanzania.

A2. **Listing is free.** Kwetuly reviews each listing before it is public and may refuse or remove a listing.

A3. **Your licences.** You confirm that your business holds every licence, registration and permit it needs (for example a business licence, and any tourism licence required for accommodation, transport or car hire under the Tourism Act, 2008), and that you will keep them valid. Kwetuly does not check this unless you buy the badge (Part B), and a listing without the badge shows "Listed: we have not checked it".

A4. **Accurate information.** Your name, type, location, photos, prices and description must be true and current. Photos must show your real place or products, and you must have the right to use them.

A5. **Private details.** Your WhatsApp number, licence document and Kwetuly's payment note are kept private: only you and Kwetuly admins can see them.

A6. **Enquiries and order requests.** Travellers can send you enquiries. Curio, gem and gear-rental shops can receive order requests (delivery to a hotel, or held for collection). You must confirm the price and how to pay before sending anything. **Travellers pay you directly; Kwetuly does not take payment for your goods or services** **[v0.2 DRAFT]** (except deposits on trip offers and group trips under Part D). You are responsible for the goods, services, receipts, delivery, refunds and any legal documents (for example gemstone certificates or export papers).

A7. **Travellers' data.** Use travellers' contact details only to answer them and serve them. Do not add them to marketing lists without their consent, share or sell them. You are responsible for your own compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

A8. **Conduct.** No fake reviews, misleading prices, bait-and-switch, harassment or discrimination. Do not say or suggest that Kwetuly recommends you.

A9. **Licence to Kwetuly.** You keep ownership of your content and allow Kwetuly to show it on Kwetuly and in Kwetuly's marketing while your listing is active.

A10. **Ending.** You can remove your listing at any time. Kwetuly may hide or remove it if you break these terms, after serious complaints, or if information turns out to be false. Kwetuly will tell you why unless there is an urgent safety reason. **[v0.2 DRAFT]** Trips with deposits that you have already accepted must still be run or cancelled under Part D.

A11. **Sample listings.** Before launch, Kwetuly showed some sample listings built from public information, marked "Sample listing". They are removed before public launch. If your business appeared as a sample and you want it changed or removed, contact [CONTACT].

> Drafting note: sample listings used real businesses' public information (names, photos?) without their agreement. They are labelled and enquiries go to Kwetuly, but the advocate should confirm this is acceptable for an invite-only phase (PDPA where sole traders are named; copyright in photos; Fair Competition Act misleading conduct). # Part B. The "Checked by Kwetuly" badge

B1. **What it is.** A yearly, paid check of your business by Kwetuly. The badge shows on your listing **with the list of what Kwetuly checked and the date**, and the date it ends.

B2. **What it is not.** The badge is **not a recommendation**, not a rating, and not a guarantee of quality, safety or service. Kwetuly will always show travellers this explanation next to the badge: "Businesses pay a yearly fee for this check. It shows what we looked at. It is not a recommendation." You must not describe the badge as a recommendation, award or certification in your own marketing. "Recommended by guides" is a separate label that only comes from real guide recommendations and cannot be bought.

B3. **What Kwetuly checks.** Kwetuly checks only the items listed below that apply, and shows only the ones that passed:
- (a) your business licence (Kwetuly looks at the document and that it is in date; Kwetuly does not confirm it with the issuing authority unless stated);
- (b) the owner's or manager's identity;
- (c) that your photos match the place, by video call or a visit (the badge says which);
- [(d) any other check Walter approves; each check must be described in plain words].

> Drafting note: the business page currently promises "We check your business licence, the owner's identity and that your photos match the place, by video call or a visit." The badge display must say "video call" or "visit" for each badge, because they are different strengths of check. The admin stores `badge_checks`, `badge_checked_at` and `badge_valid_until`.

B4. **Fee.** The yearly fee is [AMOUNT, currently "ask Kwetuly": Walter to set] [including / excluding VAT]. Kwetuly will contact you on WhatsApp to arrange the check and the payment and will give you an invoice/receipt. The fee is the same for every business of the same type [Walter to confirm] and does not depend on passing.

B5. **If a check does not pass.** If one item cannot be confirmed, Kwetuly shows only the items that passed. If no item passes, there is no badge and [Kwetuly refunds [X]% of the fee / the fee is not refunded because the check was done: Walter and advocate to decide]. Kwetuly will tell you what was missing so you can ask for a new check.

B6. **How long it lasts.** One year from the check date. After that, the badge disappears unless you renew and pass a new check. No automatic renewal or charge.

B7. **Changes during the year.** Tell Kwetuly within [14] days if your licence lapses, the owner changes, or the place changes significantly. Kwetuly may remove the badge early (without refund for the remaining time [advocate to confirm fairness]) if information was false, the licence lapses, or there are serious, credible complaints about the items checked. Kwetuly will tell you why.

B8. **No influence on ranking.** Having the badge [does / does not] change where your listing appears. [Walter to decide; if it does, travellers must be told, e.g. "Checked businesses are shown first".]

B9. **Your information.** Documents you give for the check stay private (owner and admins only) and are kept as set out in the Privacy Policy.

# Part C. Referral commissions

C1. **Separate written agreement.** Commissions only apply if you and Kwetuly sign a referral commission agreement (Schedule 1). Each business has its own agreement.

C2. **How it works.** Approved guides have a personal referral code (for example "RFxxxxxx", also as a QR code). When a traveller shows a code and buys from you, you record the sale in your Kwetuly dashboard (date, amount in TZS, code, optional note). Kwetuly invoices you monthly for the agreed share. The guide's share comes out of Kwetuly's total share [or is paid by you separately: to confirm].

C3. **Disclosure to travellers.** While the agreement is active, your listing shows: "Kwetuly and guides may earn a commission when you buy here." You agree to this note. You must not hide it or tell travellers there is no commission.

C4. **No higher prices for referred travellers.** You must not charge travellers who use a code more than you would charge them without one. [Walter to confirm this is the rule.]

C5. **Honest reporting.** You must report every referred sale honestly and on time, keep your sales records for [PERIOD], and let Kwetuly check them on reasonable notice [advocate: is an audit right appropriate for small shops?].

C6. **No pressure.** Neither you nor the guide may pressure travellers to buy. Kwetuly may end the agreement if travellers complain of pressure or misleading sales.

C7. **Tax.** Each party handles its own taxes. Kwetuly's invoices will show [VAT if registered]. [Withholding tax treatment: advocate/accountant to confirm.]

# Part D. [v0.2 DRAFT, new] Deposits on trip offers and group trips

> Drafting note [v0.2]: Part D only matters for businesses that make offers on trip requests or run group trips (for example tour companies). It mirrors guide terms 7.3, 7.6 and 7.7. Deposits are off until the company is registered, the advocate has answered, and Walter says go.

D1. **When it applies.** If you set a deposit percentage on an offer or group trip, the traveller pays that deposit through Kwetuly's payment provider (Selcom), by card or mobile money. The Deposit and Refund Policy applies to those deposits, and these terms explain your side of it.

D2. **When travellers can pay.** Travellers can pay a deposit up to 8 days before the start date (Tanzania date). After that they pay you directly.

D3. **Your cancellation terms.** Show your own cancellation terms on every offer and group trip. They decide how much of a deposit you keep when a traveller cancels. If you show none, the default terms apply: 30 days or more before the start, you keep 0%; 8 to 29 days, you keep 50%; 7 days or less, or a no-show, you keep 100%.

D4. **Kwetuly's charge.** Kwetuly takes no commission on deposits [Walter to confirm]. When a traveller cancels, Kwetuly keeps a cancellation charge of 10% of the deposit (at most TSh [CAP]) out of the money that would otherwise go back to the traveller. It is not taken from your share. Travellers see this charge before they pay.

D5. **If you cancel.**
- (a) The traveller gets 100% of the deposit back if you cancel, cannot provide the trip as agreed, change the price, dates or main itinerary and the traveller does not accept, or Kwetuly takes the trip down because of something you did.
- (b) If Kwetuly has already passed that deposit to you, Kwetuly refunds the traveller and recovers the amount from you [from your next payout / by invoice: Walter and advocate to decide].
- (c) Kwetuly may also deduct from your next payout the payment and refund fees it actually paid on that deposit, up to 10% of the deposit. Before deducting, Kwetuly shows you the amount and how it was worked out, and you can question it. A Kwetuly manager approves every deduction.
- (d) Kwetuly does **not** recover fees when the cause is outside anyone's control (for example a park closure, natural disaster or government ban), or when a group trip did not reach its minimum number.
- (e) If Kwetuly cancels a trip because of fraud or a serious breach by you, (b) and (c) apply.
- [(f) If you have no later payout: [Kwetuly invoices you / the fees are not recovered]: Walter and advocate to decide.]

D6. **Payouts on hold.** Kwetuly does not pass a deposit to you while a refund request or card dispute is open on it, or after the trip is cancelled or the traveller's spot is declined. After a traveller cancels, only your share under D3 is passed to you.

D7. **Payout details.** Payout details must be in the business's name or the owner's own name. Kwetuly may ask for ID and business registration before the first payout.
