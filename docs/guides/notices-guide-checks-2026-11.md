- Files: a clear photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, up to 15 MB each. The whole card must show, the name and the dates must be readable, and there should be no glare.

## Short replies for common questions

**"Can I send my licence on WhatsApp?"**
- EN: Thank you, but please do not send documents here. Upload them in "Your documents" on kwetuly.com or in the app, so they stay private. Only you and Kwetuly see them there.
- SW: Asante, lakini tafadhali usitume nyaraka hapa. Zipakie kwenye "Nyaraka zako" kwenye kwetuly.com au app, ili zibaki siri. Huko ni wewe na Kwetuly tu mnaoziona.

**Documents in chat (a guide already sent one).** Delete the photo first, then reply:
- EN: Thank you. For your privacy we have deleted the photo from this chat. Please upload it in "Your documents" on kwetuly.com or in the app instead.
- SW: Asante. Kwa ajili ya usiri wako tumefuta picha hiyo kwenye mazungumzo haya. Tafadhali ipakie kwenye "Nyaraka zako" kwenye kwetuly.com au app badala yake.

**"I do not have a first aid certificate."**
- EN: Your profile can go live once we have checked a current first aid or WFR certificate. Ask your guide association or guide school where the next course is. Your profile and packages are kept until then.
- SW: Wasifu wako unaweza kuonekana baada ya kukagua cheti cha huduma ya kwanza au WFR kilicho hai. Uliza chama chako cha waongozaji au chuo chako kozi inayofuata ni lini na wapi. Wasifu na vifurushi vyako vinahifadhiwa hadi hapo.

(Do not name a course provider or price until one has been confirmed.)

**"Will this get me more bookings?"**
- EN: We cannot promise bookings. What changes is that travellers can see what Kwetuly checked and when.
- SW: Hatuwezi kuahidi safari. Kinachobadilika ni kwamba wasafiri wanaona Kwetuly ilikagua nini na lini.

**"My car belongs to the company. Do I need the driving licence?"**
- EN: Yes, if you drive guests, in any car. If you only guide and someone else drives, leave "I drive guests" off.
- SW: Ndiyo, kama unaendesha wageni, kwa gari lolote. Kama unaongoza tu na mtu mwingine anaendesha, usiwashe "Ninaendesha wageni".

(Do not say which licence class is needed. Legal is still confirming it, plan section 7.)
