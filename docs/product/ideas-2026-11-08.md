- **Size:** S = days, M = 1 to 3 weeks, L = a month or more.
- **Money:** how it could make money for Kwetuly. "None" is a valid answer. No prices are given here: business-manager proposes, Walter sets.
- **Risks:** "legal" and "payments" mark where legal or payments-engineer must check before any build. "To verify" means nobody has confirmed it from an official source yet.
