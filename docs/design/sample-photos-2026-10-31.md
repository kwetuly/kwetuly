## Hostel rename (decision for Walter)

"Kahawa Bunk Hostel" becomes **Mzunguko Bunk Hostel**. Mzunguko is Kiswahili for "circuit" or "round trip", which fits a hostel where travellers team up for the northern safari circuit, and it has no overlap with Kahawa House or any other name in the list.

Web check on 2026-10-31: the exact name "Mzunguko Bunk Hostel" in quotes returned no results on DuckDuckGo and nothing relevant on Bing. Broader searches ("Mzunguko" with hostel, lodge or Arusha) were blocked by the search engines' bot checks, so they could not be completed. Mzunguko is an everyday word (it also means roundabout), so it may appear in street or project names; it was not found as a business name. Like the other names, this is a web check only, not a BRELA register search.

Dropped while checking: "Mkoba Bunk Hostel" (mkoba = bag; too close to Vodacom's M-Koba service) and "Bunk na Chai Hostel" (search gave no usable result, and the name mixes English and Kiswahili awkwardly).

## How the photos were found

- Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay all refuse automated access from here (Unsplash asks for an API key; Pexels and Pixabay return a bot check). So every photo below comes from **Wikimedia Commons**, filtered to CC0 and CC BY only, at least 1600 px wide.
- Some Commons files are old Unsplash photos imported under CC0 (the Unsplash licence before June 2017). They are marked "originally on Unsplash" in the credit line. The Commons page is the licence record to keep.
- Each chosen photo was checked by eye on a small preview (360 px thumbnails, kept only in a temporary folder) for faces, children, logos, signs and readable names. Things to re-check at full size are in the notes.
- Facts about the photo (where it was taken) come from the Commons description. Alt texts never name a place the photo does not show (for example, a Kenyan bird photo does not say "Tarangire").

## Rules for step 3 (devops, backend-engineer, frontend-engineer)

- Download the "Direct image" URL, crop as written, export WebP at the size given, and **strip all EXIF and location data**.
- Store the "Credit line to store" with each image (CC BY needs it; CC0 does not, but we keep it anyway). Show it in small grey text under the photo or in a "Photo credits" line on the page.
- Keep the source page URL with each image in the database, so we can prove the licence later.
- Expected size: about 94 images at roughly 200 to 300 KB each, so about 20 to 28 MB.
- Every sample card and page keeps its sample label. These are stock photos of other places, never photos of the business.
