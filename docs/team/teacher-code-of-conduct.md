## Part A – English

### 1. Who this is for

1.1 This code applies to every Kwetuly guide who offers an online lesson package ("you", "teacher"), and to every lesson, message and contact with a learner that starts on Kwetuly.

1.2 You are an independent teacher, not a Kwetuly employee. You set your lesson content, price and schedule within these rules. **[Advocate]** Whether Kwetuly's rules and approval powers could make teachers look like employees under the Employment and Labour Relations Act must be checked.

1.3 When a learner books your lesson, the agreement for the lesson is between you and the learner. Kwetuly provides the listing, booking requests and messages. **[Advocate]** to confirm who counts as the seller while payment is direct.

### 2. Professional behaviour

2.1 Be on time, prepared and in a quiet place with a working internet connection, camera and microphone.

2.2 Dress and present yourself as you would for a professional class. Keep your background neutral and appropriate.

2.3 Be respectful and patient with every learner, whatever their country, language level, age (18+), sex, religion, background, disability or any other personal trait. No insults, mocking or discrimination.

2.4 Keep lessons about the subject you advertised. Do not use lessons for religious or political persuasion, selling unrelated products, or recruiting learners for other businesses.

2.5 No alcohol or drugs before or during a lesson.

2.6 No sexual comments, jokes, images, gestures or requests of any kind, before, during or after a lesson.

### 3. No harassment and no contact pressure

3.1 Contact details are shown only after you accept a booking. Use them only to arrange and give that learner's lessons.

3.2 Do not:
- push a learner to move to WhatsApp, social media or another app;
- ask for or follow their personal social-media accounts;
- send romantic or flirting messages, or ask to meet in person;
- keep messaging a learner who has not replied or has said no;
- ask for gifts, loans, tips beyond your listed price, or help with visas or travel;
- contact a learner after they have asked you to stop, or after Kwetuly has told you to stop.

3.3 You may offer more lessons through Kwetuly. You must not take a learner off Kwetuly to avoid Kwetuly's rules or reviews. **[Advocate]** to confirm this limit is reasonable and not an unfair term.

3.4 If a learner harasses you, stop the lesson politely and report it (section 10). You never have to continue a lesson where you feel unsafe or disrespected.

### 4. Privacy and recording

4.1 **Kwetuly does not record lessons and does not store lesson content.**

4.2 Do not record a lesson (video, audio or screen), or take screenshots or photos that show the learner, unless the learner agrees **in writing in Kwetuly messages before the lesson**. The learner can say no without any effect on the lesson. If they agree, use the recording only for that learner's learning, share it only with them, and delete it when they ask.

4.3 Never post a learner's name, face, voice, messages or reviews on social media, in marketing or anywhere public without their separate written consent.

4.4 Learners' data (name, contact details, time zone, country, anything they tell you) is private. Do not share, sell or reuse it, add it to mailing lists, or keep it longer than you need for their lessons. **[Advocate]** Teachers may have their own duties under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (as already noted in the Terms for Guides); the advocate should confirm what applies.

4.5 Do not ask learners for ID documents, passport numbers, card details, bank passwords or health information. If a cooking learner tells you about an allergy, use it only to keep that lesson safe and do not write it anywhere shared.

4.6 Protect your own devices: use a password or PIN, and do not let other people read your Kwetuly messages.

### 5. Safe video links only

5.1 Use only a video link that starts with `https://` and is from Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Jitsi or Whereby. Kwetuly's form will refuse other links.

5.2 Create the meeting from your own account on that service. Send the link only through Kwetuly, after you accept the booking.

5.3 Never send shortened links, links to download files or apps, payment links disguised as lesson links, or anything that asks the learner to sign in to "Kwetuly" outside the Kwetuly site or app.

5.4 Use the service's waiting room or password where it has one, and admit only your booked learner.

5.5 If your link stops working, send a new allowed link through Kwetuly messages. Do not switch to another service by phone message.

### 6. Honest lessons and honest profiles

6.1 Describe each lesson package truthfully: subject, level, length in minutes, what the learner will do, what they need (for cooking: ingredients and equipment) and the price.

6.2 State only skills and qualifications you really have. A certificate (for example from BAKITA, a university or a cooking school) shows on your profile only if Kwetuly has seen it, and the profile says when it was checked. Never say or suggest that Kwetuly recommends, certifies or guarantees you.

6.3 Do not promise results you cannot control, for example "fluent in 4 weeks" or "pass any exam".

6.4 Use only teaching materials, recipes, photos and music you created or have the right to use. **[Advocate]** to confirm ownership of lesson materials and whether Kwetuly may use any clip in marketing (only ever with separate consent).

6.5 Reviews: do not write your own, ask friends to write them, pay for them or pressure learners. Reply to bad reviews politely.

### 7. Cooking lessons: food safety and allergies

7.1 List **every ingredient** in the lesson package and send the recipe in advance, so learners can check for allergies and buy what they need. Mark common allergens clearly, for example peanuts and other nuts, sesame, milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, wheat (gluten) and soy.

7.2 At the start of each cooking lesson, ask: "Is there any ingredient you cannot eat or want to replace?" Suggest safe swaps only if you know them; otherwise say so and leave the ingredient out.

7.3 Give clear safety reminders while cooking: knife handling, hot oil, steam and open flames, keeping children and pets away from the stove, raw meat and fish hygiene, cooking meat and fish through, washing hands and surfaces.

7.4 Do not make health, medical, weight-loss or nutrition claims about any dish or ingredient. Do not give medical advice.

7.5 If a learner seems hurt, burnt or unwell during a lesson, stop the lesson, tell them to call their local emergency number, and report it to Kwetuly. Do not try to treat them by video.

7.6 Kwetuly does not sell or ship food, and neither do teachers through Kwetuly. Do not send food, spices or ingredients to learners through a Kwetuly booking.

7.7 **[Advocate]** The research notes say that allergy safety is the learner's responsibility under the learner terms. This code still asks teachers to list ingredients and ask about allergies; the advocate should confirm how teacher duties and the learner terms fit together.

### 8. Prices, payment, cancellations and no-shows

8.1 **Payment for now.** Kwetuly does not take payment for lessons yet. The learner pays you directly, as agreed in Kwetuly messages. Charge only the price shown on your lesson package. Never ask for payment to an account that pretends to be Kwetuly. Give the learner a clear receipt. You are responsible for your own tax. **[Advocate]** Payment rules, receipts and tax on direct payments must be checked (see research file, sections 2.4 and 3.2).

8.2 **Your cancellation terms.** Each lesson package must state its cancellation terms. Unless Walter approves different default terms, use these drafts:
- Learner cancels at least [24] hours before: full refund of anything they paid, or a free reschedule.
- Learner cancels less than [24] hours before, or does not join within [15] minutes: you may keep [up to 50% / the full price – Walter to decide] if your package says so.
- **You cancel, or you do not join within [10] minutes:** the learner gets a full refund of anything they paid for that lesson, within [7] days, or a new time if they prefer.
- Technical failure on your side that ends the lesson early: offer the missing time or a refund for it.

8.3 Cancel through Kwetuly, not only by phone, so the record is clear. Learners are in different time zones: always confirm the time in both East Africa Time and the learner's time zone.

8.4 If you cancel or miss [2] lessons in [30] days without a good reason, Kwetuly may hide your lesson packages (section 11).

8.5 **[Advocate]** Kwetuly cannot refund money it never received. The advocate should confirm whether these default terms fit the Electronic Transactions Act and Fair Competition Act rules mentioned in the research file, and who owes a refund while payment is direct.

### 9. If a learner turns out to be under 18

9.1 Lessons on Kwetuly are for adults (18+) only. Every learner confirms "I am 18 or older" when booking.

9.2 If a learner tells you they are under 18, or you have a strong reason to think so (for example they say they are in primary or secondary school, or a parent says so):
1. End the lesson politely and calmly. Do not accuse or question them. You can say: "I'm sorry, Kwetuly lessons are for adults only, so I have to stop here. Kwetuly will be in touch."
2. Do not continue contact by any channel, and do not offer lessons outside Kwetuly.
3. Do not take or keep any recording, screenshot or photo, and do not ask for proof of age.
4. Report it to Kwetuly within [24 hours] through the report button or [SAFETY CONTACT].
5. Do not ask for or take payment for that lesson; if you were already paid, refund it [as Kwetuly tells you].

9.3 Kwetuly will pause the learner's account while it looks into the report. You will not be penalised for reporting in good faith, even if the learner turns out to be an adult.

9.4 Any sexual content, sexual message or grooming involving a child is a crime. If you see or receive anything like that, do not save, copy or forward it; report it to Kwetuly at once. **[Advocate]** The research file lists the Cybercrimes Act, 2015 (s. 13), the Law of the Child Act, 2009, the Child Protection Laws (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act, 2024 (title to be confirmed) and the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. The advocate must confirm what each requires, including any duty to report to the authorities. Only Walter decides when Kwetuly contacts authorities.

### 10. How to report a problem

10.1 Teachers and learners can report a lesson, a message or a person with the report button in Kwetuly, or by writing to [SAFETY CONTACT].

10.2 Report within [24 hours]:
- harassment, threats or sexual behaviour;
- a learner who may be under 18;
- an injury or health emergency during a cooking lesson;
- an unsafe or fake link, a scam, or someone pretending to be Kwetuly;
- a learner or teacher sharing someone's data or recordings without consent.

10.3 In an emergency, contact the local emergency services first, then Kwetuly.

10.4 Say what happened, when, and which booking it was. Do not attach ID documents, health details or explicit images. Kwetuly will ask for what it needs.

10.5 Kwetuly will confirm it received the report within [2 working days], keep it private to the people who need to handle it, and tell you the outcome where it can. No one may be punished for reporting in good faith. False reports made on purpose break this code.

### 11. Steps if the rules are broken

11.1 Kwetuly looks at the facts, tells you what was reported (without exposing the reporter where that would put them at risk) and gives you a chance to explain, unless there is an urgent safety risk.

11.2 Depending on how serious it is, Kwetuly may:
1. **Reminder or written warning** – for minor or first problems (for example late once, unclear package description).
2. **Hide lesson packages** until a fix is made – for repeated lateness or no-shows, misleading descriptions, missing ingredient lists.
3. **Suspend** the teacher's lessons or whole profile while Kwetuly looks into a serious report.
4. **Remove** the teacher from lessons or from Kwetuly, and remove any checks shown on the profile.

11.3 **Immediate suspension** (step 3 straight away) for: sexual behaviour or harassment; any contact with a child of a sexual nature; continuing contact with a learner known to be under 18; threats or violence; fraud, fake links or pretending to be Kwetuly; recording or sharing a learner without consent; serious food-safety negligence that hurt someone.

11.4 Walter makes every suspension and removal decision. Kwetuly keeps a private record of reports and decisions, with no more personal data than needed. **[Advocate]** to confirm the fairness of this process, how long records are kept, and how it fits with the Terms for Guides section 9.

11.5 You may ask Walter to review a decision once, in writing, within [14] days.

### 12. Your agreement

By ticking "I accept the teaching rules" you confirm you have read this code, will follow it, and understand that breaking it can lead to the steps in section 11.

# Kanuni za Maadili za Kwetuly kwa Walimu wa Mtandaoni

**Msingi wa uamuzi wa Walter (30-10-2026):**
- Walimu ni waongoza watalii walioidhinishwa na Kwetuly wanaotoa vifurushi vya masomo (Kiswahili au mapishi ya Kitanzania/Kiafrika, kwa video).
- Wanafunzi ni watu wazima (miaka 18 au zaidi) tu.
- Masomo hufanyika kwa kiungo cha video kinachoanza na `https://` kutoka huduma zinazoruhusiwa: Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Jitsi au Whereby.
- Mpaka Kwetuly iwe na malipo ya mtandaoni, mwanafunzi humlipa mwalimu moja kwa moja.

### 1. Kanuni hizi ni za nani

1.1 Kanuni hizi zinamhusu kila mwongozaji wa Kwetuly anayetoa kifurushi cha somo la mtandaoni ("wewe", "mwalimu"), na kila somo, ujumbe na mawasiliano na mwanafunzi yanayoanzia Kwetuly.

1.2 Wewe ni mwalimu huru, si mfanyakazi wa Kwetuly. Unaamua maudhui ya somo, bei na ratiba ndani ya kanuni hizi. **[Wakili]** Ihakikishwe kama kanuni na mamlaka ya Kwetuly ya kuidhinisha yanaweza kuwafanya walimu waonekane kama wafanyakazi chini ya Sheria ya Ajira na Mahusiano Kazini.

1.3 Mwanafunzi akiweka nafasi ya somo lako, makubaliano ya somo ni kati yako na mwanafunzi. Kwetuly inatoa tangazo, maombi ya nafasi na ujumbe. **[Wakili]** athibitishe nani ni muuzaji wakati malipo ni ya moja kwa moja.

### 2. Tabia ya kitaaluma

2.1 Fika kwa wakati, ukiwa umejiandaa, mahali patulivu, na intaneti, kamera na kipaza sauti vinavyofanya kazi.

2.2 Vaa na jionyeshe kama ungefanya kwenye darasa rasmi. Mandhari ya nyuma yawe ya kawaida na yanayofaa.

2.3 Mheshimu na kumvumilia kila mwanafunzi, bila kujali nchi, kiwango cha lugha, umri (18+), jinsia, dini, asili, ulemavu au sifa nyingine yoyote binafsi. Hakuna matusi, dhihaka wala ubaguzi.

2.4 Somo libaki kwenye mada uliyotangaza. Usitumie masomo kushawishi kidini au kisiasa, kuuza bidhaa zisizohusiana, au kuwavuta wanafunzi kwa biashara nyingine.

2.5 Usinywe pombe wala kutumia dawa za kulevya kabla au wakati wa somo.

2.6 Hakuna maneno, utani, picha, ishara au maombi ya kingono kwa namna yoyote, kabla, wakati au baada ya somo.

### 3. Hakuna unyanyasaji wala kulazimisha mawasiliano

3.1 Taarifa za mawasiliano huonekana tu baada ya kukubali nafasi. Zitumie tu kupanga na kufundisha masomo ya mwanafunzi huyo.

3.2 Usifanye yafuatayo:
- kumshinikiza mwanafunzi kuhamia WhatsApp, mitandao ya kijamii au programu nyingine;
- kuomba au kufuatilia akaunti zake binafsi za mitandao ya kijamii;
- kutuma ujumbe wa kimapenzi au wa kutongoza, au kuomba kukutana ana kwa ana;
- kuendelea kumtumia ujumbe mwanafunzi ambaye hajajibu au amekataa;
- kuomba zawadi, mikopo, bakshishi zaidi ya bei uliyoweka, au msaada wa viza au safari;
- kuwasiliana na mwanafunzi baada ya kukuomba uache, au baada ya Kwetuly kukuambia uache.

3.3 Unaweza kutoa masomo zaidi kupitia Kwetuly. Usimtoe mwanafunzi nje ya Kwetuly ili kukwepa kanuni au maoni ya Kwetuly. **[Wakili]** athibitishe kwamba kizuizi hiki ni cha haki.

3.4 Mwanafunzi akikunyanyasa, sitisha somo kwa adabu na uripoti (sehemu ya 10). Huna wajibu wa kuendelea na somo ambapo unajisikia si salama au hauheshimiwi.

### 4. Faragha na kurekodi

4.1 **Kwetuly hairekodi masomo na haihifadhi maudhui ya masomo.**

4.2 Usirekodi somo (video, sauti au skrini), wala kupiga picha au picha za skrini zinazomwonyesha mwanafunzi, isipokuwa mwanafunzi amekubali **kwa maandishi kwenye ujumbe wa Kwetuly kabla ya somo**. Mwanafunzi anaweza kukataa bila athari yoyote kwa somo. Akikubali, tumia rekodi kwa ajili ya kujifunza kwake tu, mshirikishe yeye tu, na ifute akiomba.

4.3 Usichapishe jina, sura, sauti, ujumbe au maoni ya mwanafunzi kwenye mitandao ya kijamii, matangazo au mahali popote pa wazi bila ridhaa yake ya maandishi tofauti.

4.4 Taarifa za mwanafunzi (jina, mawasiliano, saa za eneo, nchi, chochote anachokuambia) ni siri. Usizishiriki, kuziuza wala kuzitumia upya, usiziweke kwenye orodha za barua pepe, wala kuzihifadhi zaidi ya muda unaohitajika kwa masomo yake. **[Wakili]** Walimu wanaweza kuwa na wajibu wao wenyewe chini ya Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi, 2022; wakili athibitishe.

4.5 Usiwaombe wanafunzi vitambulisho, namba za pasipoti, taarifa za kadi, nywila za benki au taarifa za afya. Mwanafunzi wa mapishi akikuambia kuhusu mzio (aleji), itumie tu kufanya somo hilo kuwa salama na usiiandike mahali panaposhirikiwa.

4.6 Linda vifaa vyako: tumia nywila au PIN, na usiruhusu watu wengine kusoma ujumbe wako wa Kwetuly.

### 5. Viungo salama vya video tu

5.1 Tumia kiungo cha video kinachoanza na `https://` kutoka Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Jitsi au Whereby tu. Fomu ya Kwetuly itakataa viungo vingine.

5.2 Tengeneza mkutano kutoka akaunti yako mwenyewe kwenye huduma hiyo. Tuma kiungo kupitia Kwetuly tu, baada ya kukubali nafasi.

5.3 Usitume kamwe viungo vilivyofupishwa, viungo vya kupakua faili au programu, viungo vya malipo vilivyojificha kama viungo vya somo, au chochote kinachomwomba mwanafunzi aingie "Kwetuly" nje ya tovuti au programu ya Kwetuly.

5.4 Tumia chumba cha kusubiri au nywila ya huduma pale inapopatikana, na umruhusu kuingia mwanafunzi aliyeweka nafasi tu.

5.5 Kiungo chako kikiacha kufanya kazi, tuma kiungo kipya kinachoruhusiwa kupitia ujumbe wa Kwetuly. Usihamie huduma nyingine kwa ujumbe wa simu.

### 6. Masomo na wasifu wa kweli

6.1 Eleza kila kifurushi cha somo kwa ukweli: mada, kiwango, urefu kwa dakika, mwanafunzi atafanya nini, anahitaji nini (kwa mapishi: viungo vya chakula na vifaa) na bei.

6.2 Taja ujuzi na sifa ulizo nazo kweli tu. Cheti (kwa mfano kutoka BAKITA, chuo kikuu au shule ya mapishi) kinaonekana kwenye wasifu wako tu kama Kwetuly imekiona, na wasifu unaonyesha lini kilikaguliwa. Usiseme wala kudokeza kwamba Kwetuly inakupendekeza, inakuthibitisha au inakuhakikishia.

6.3 Usiahidi matokeo usiyoweza kuyadhibiti, kwa mfano "utaongea kwa ufasaha ndani ya wiki 4" au "utafaulu mtihani wowote".

6.4 Tumia tu vifaa vya kufundishia, mapishi, picha na muziki uliotengeneza mwenyewe au una haki ya kuvitumia. **[Wakili]** athibitishe umiliki wa vifaa vya masomo na kama Kwetuly inaweza kutumia kipande chochote katika matangazo (daima kwa ridhaa tofauti tu).

6.5 Maoni (reviews): usijiandikie, usiwaombe marafiki waandike, usilipe wala kuwashinikiza wanafunzi. Jibu maoni mabaya kwa adabu.

### 7. Masomo ya mapishi: usalama wa chakula na mzio

7.1 Orodhesha **kila kiungo cha chakula** kwenye kifurushi na tuma mapishi mapema, ili wanafunzi waangalie mzio na wanunue wanachohitaji. Weka alama wazi kwenye vyakula vinavyosababisha mzio mara nyingi, kwa mfano karanga na njugu nyingine, ufuta, maziwa, mayai, samaki, samakigamba, ngano (gluteni) na soya.

7.2 Mwanzoni mwa kila somo la mapishi, uliza: "Kuna kiungo chochote usichoweza kula au unachotaka kubadilisha?" Pendekeza mbadala salama tu kama unaujua; vinginevyo sema hivyo na uache kiungo hicho.

7.3 Toa tahadhari wazi wakati wa kupika: matumizi ya kisu, mafuta ya moto, mvuke na moto, kuwaweka watoto na wanyama mbali na jiko, usafi wa nyama na samaki mbichi, kupika nyama na samaki hadi viive, kunawa mikono na kusafisha sehemu za kazi.

7.4 Usitoe madai ya kiafya, kitabibu, kupunguza uzito au lishe kuhusu chakula au kiungo chochote. Usitoe ushauri wa kitabibu.

7.5 Mwanafunzi akionekana kuumia, kuungua au kuugua wakati wa somo, sitisha somo, mwambie apige namba ya dharura ya eneo lake, na uripoti kwa Kwetuly. Usijaribu kumtibu kwa video.

7.6 Kwetuly haiuzi wala haisafirishi chakula, na walimu pia hawafanyi hivyo kupitia Kwetuly. Usitume chakula, viungo au bidhaa kwa wanafunzi kupitia nafasi ya Kwetuly.

7.7 **[Wakili]** athibitishe jinsi wajibu wa mwalimu kuhusu mzio unavyoendana na masharti ya mwanafunzi.

### 8. Bei, malipo, kughairi na kutokufika

8.1 **Malipo kwa sasa.** Kwetuly bado haipokei malipo ya masomo. Mwanafunzi anakulipa moja kwa moja, kama mlivyokubaliana kwenye ujumbe wa Kwetuly. Toza bei iliyoonyeshwa kwenye kifurushi chako tu. Usiombe kamwe malipo kwenye akaunti inayojifanya kuwa ya Kwetuly. Mpe mwanafunzi risiti iliyo wazi. Unawajibika kwa kodi yako mwenyewe. **[Wakili]** Kanuni za malipo, risiti na kodi kwa malipo ya moja kwa moja zihakikiwe.

8.2 **Masharti yako ya kughairi.** Kila kifurushi lazima kieleze masharti yake ya kughairi. Isipokuwa Walter aidhinishe masharti mengine, tumia rasimu hii:
- Mwanafunzi akighairi angalau saa [24] kabla: arudishiwe chochote alicholipa chote, au apangiwe muda mwingine bila malipo.
- Mwanafunzi akighairi chini ya saa [24] kabla, au asipoingia ndani ya dakika [15]: unaweza kubaki na [hadi 50% / bei yote – Walter aamue] kama kifurushi chako kinasema hivyo.
- **Ukighairi wewe, au usipoingia ndani ya dakika [10]:** mwanafunzi arudishiwe chochote alicholipa kwa somo hilo chote, ndani ya siku [7], au apangiwe muda mwingine akipenda.
- Tatizo la kiufundi upande wako linalomaliza somo mapema: toa muda uliopungua au rudisha malipo ya muda huo.

8.3 Ghairi kupitia Kwetuly, si kwa simu tu, ili kumbukumbu iwe wazi. Wanafunzi wako katika saa za maeneo tofauti: thibitisha muda kila mara kwa Saa za Afrika Mashariki na kwa saa za eneo la mwanafunzi.

8.4 Ukighairi au ukikosa masomo [2] ndani ya siku [30] bila sababu nzuri, Kwetuly inaweza kuficha vifurushi vyako vya masomo (sehemu ya 11).

8.5 **[Wakili]** Kwetuly haiwezi kurudisha pesa ambayo haikupokea. Wakili athibitishe kama masharti haya yanaendana na sheria za miamala ya kielektroniki na ushindani wa haki, na nani anadaiwa kurudisha pesa wakati malipo ni ya moja kwa moja.

### 9. Ikiwa mwanafunzi ana umri chini ya miaka 18

9.1 Masomo ya Kwetuly ni kwa watu wazima (18+) tu. Kila mwanafunzi anathibitisha "Nina umri wa miaka 18 au zaidi" anapoweka nafasi.

9.2 Mwanafunzi akikuambia ana umri chini ya miaka 18, au ukiwa na sababu nzito ya kuamini hivyo (kwa mfano anasema yuko shule ya msingi au sekondari, au mzazi anasema hivyo):

### C2. Interview questions (30–40 minutes, same questions for every candidate)

1. Tell us about your teaching or cooking experience. Who have you taught, and for how long?
2. *(Kiswahili)* A beginner asks why we say "ninakula" but "sili". How would you explain it? *(Cooking)* Walk us through the dish in your package. Which steps are hard for a beginner at home abroad, and how would you help?
3. A learner's internet keeps dropping and they are frustrated. What do you do?
4. During a lesson, a learner mentions they are in Form 3 at school. What do you do, step by step?
5. A learner asks to record the lesson so they can watch it later. What do you say and do?
6. After a few lessons, a learner keeps sending you personal messages late at night and asks to talk on WhatsApp instead. How do you handle it?
7. *(Cooking)* A learner says they are allergic to peanuts and your recipe uses groundnut sauce. What do you do? What if they burn their hand during the lesson?
8. Your Zoom link stops working five minutes before the lesson. What do you do?
9. You are ill and cannot teach tomorrow's lesson. What do you do, and what happens to the learner's payment?
10. How would you describe your skills on your profile so that a learner knows exactly what to expect?
11. What questions do you have about Kwetuly or these rules?
