## Part A – English

### 1. Who this is for

1.1 This code applies to every Kwetuly guide who offers a VIP personal trip host service or an investment visit host service ("you", "host"), and to every trip, meeting, message and contact with a client ("client") that starts on Kwetuly.

1.2 You are an independent host, not a Kwetuly employee. You set your day rate, plans and schedule within these rules. **[Advocate]** Whether Kwetuly's rules and approval powers could make hosts look like employees under the Employment and Labour Relations Act must be checked.

1.3 When a client books you, the agreement for the service is between you and the client. Kwetuly provides the listing, booking requests and messages. **[Advocate]** to confirm who counts as the seller, and whether planning a full trip for a client needs a tour operator or travel agent licence (Tourism Act and regulations) rather than a guide licence alone.

### 2. What a host does, and what a host must never do

2.1 A host may: plan a trip or visit programme; book or suggest licensed guides, stays and activities listed on Kwetuly or with licensed businesses; accompany the client; explain local customs; interpret if they are fluent; arrange meetings the client asks for; and point to official websites and offices.

2.2 A host must **never**:
- give investment, financial, tax or legal advice, or say whether a project, company, land or share is a good investment;
- promise that a licence, permit, land title, visa, work permit or registration will be approved, or approved faster;
- offer, pay, ask for or pass on any bribe, "facilitation fee", "speed money" or gift to any public official, police officer, park ranger, border officer or employee of a state body, for a client or for themselves;
- hold, receive or move a client's investment money, deposits for land or companies, or act as their agent before a government office without the right written authority and licence;
- offer or act as security, close protection or a bodyguard, carry a weapon, or describe themselves as "security";
- drive clients as a paid transfer through Kwetuly (transfers are switched off);
- arrange anything illegal, including unlicensed hunting, wildlife products, drugs, or sexual services.

2.3 If a client asks for any of the things in 2.2, say no politely and point them to the right professional or official source. You can say: "That is outside what I am allowed to do as a Kwetuly host. For that you need a licensed [advocate / financial adviser / security company]; I can show you the official list."

2.4 **[Advocate]** to confirm: the licence a VIP host needs; whether close protection and security work is regulated and how (police or other authority); who may represent an investor before TISEZA, BRELA or other bodies; and whether an investment visit host service needs anything from the Capital Markets and Securities Authority.

### 3. Professional behaviour

3.1 Be on time, prepared, sober and dressed appropriately for the places you visit (offices, villages, parks, religious sites).

3.2 Be respectful to every client, guide, porter, driver, official and community member, whatever their country, age, sex, religion, background, disability or any other personal trait. No insults, mocking or discrimination.

3.3 No alcohol or drugs while on duty.

3.4 No sexual comments, jokes, gestures, requests or relationships with clients while you are their host.

3.5 Treat communities fairly: ask before photographing people, follow village and site rules, and do not take clients into homes or villages without the community's agreement.

3.6 Pay the people you bring in (guides, porters, cooks, drivers, translators) fairly, on time and at least [the rates in the Kwetuly guide and crew pay guidance / the lawful minimum – Walter to decide]. Do not keep their tips.

### 4. Confidentiality of client information

4.1 VIP and investment clients often need extra privacy. Everything you learn about a client is **confidential**: who they are, that they are in Tanzania, where they stay, their plans and timetable, who they meet, their business interests, money, family, health and travel documents.

4.2 Do not:
- tell anyone (including friends, family, other guides, journalists, hotels beyond what a booking needs) who your client is or where they are;
- post photos, videos, locations or "check-ins" of a client, their car, hotel or meetings, during the trip or after, without their **separate written consent**;
- share a client's business plans or the names of people they meet with anyone, including people who might compete with them or try to sell to them;
- keep copies of passports, IDs or other documents; if a booking needs a document (for example a park permit), send it straight to the official system or the licensed business, then delete your copy;
- discuss clients in group chats.

4.3 Share with guides, stays and drivers only what they need for their part of the trip (for example a first name, pick-up time and number of people).

4.4 Use Kwetuly messages for planning. Protect your phone and laptop with a password or PIN. If your phone is lost or stolen, or you think client information has leaked, tell Kwetuly within [24 hours].

4.5 Confidentiality continues after the trip ends, and after you stop being a Kwetuly host.

4.6 Confidentiality does not stop you from reporting a crime, a safety risk or a breach of this code to Kwetuly, or from doing what the law requires.

4.7 **[Advocate]** Hosts may have their own duties under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022; the advocate should confirm what applies and whether a separate confidentiality agreement (NDA) with the host or client is needed.

### 5. No gifts, no side deals, no conflicts of interest

5.1 **Gifts from clients.** You may accept a small, voluntary thank-you or tip worth no more than [USD 50 / Walter to decide] given openly. Do not ask for gifts, loans, money for family, school fees, help with visas or travel, or investment in your own projects. Report any gift above that limit to Kwetuly and decline it.

5.2 **Gifts and commissions from others.** Do not accept money, gifts, commissions or "kickbacks" from hotels, shops, land sellers, project owners, lawyers, agents or anyone else for sending a client to them, unless the arrangement is approved by Kwetuly and **told to the client in writing before** they decide. Kwetuly discloses its own commissions to travellers, and hosts must do the same.

5.3 **Side deals.** Do not:
- take a client off Kwetuly to avoid Kwetuly's rules, commission or reviews **[Advocate]** to confirm this limit is reasonable;
- sell or broker land, property, minerals, gemstones, company shares or any investment to a client, or introduce a client to a deal in which you, your family or friends have an interest;
- become a partner, employee or agent of a client's business while you are their host.

5.4 **Conflicts of interest.** If you, your family or a close friend has any interest in a business, project, piece of land or person the client wants to visit, tell the client and Kwetuly in writing before the visit. Kwetuly may ask another host to take over that part.

### 6. No payments to officials

6.1 Never offer, give, promise, ask for or accept a bribe, in any form (cash, mobile money, gifts, meals, favours, jobs), to or from any public official, police, ranger, border or customs officer, or state employee.

6.2 Pay only official, published fees, through official channels (for example park fees through the official system), and ask for an official receipt.

6.3 If an official asks you or a client for an unofficial payment: politely decline, do not pay, keep the client calm and safe, note the time, place and what was asked (no personal data of the client), and report it to Kwetuly within [24 hours]. If anyone is in danger, safety comes first.

6.4 **[Advocate]** to confirm the duties under the Prevention and Combating of Corruption Act and any duty to report to the Prevention and Combating of Corruption Bureau (PCCB). Only Walter decides when Kwetuly contacts authorities.

### 7. Investment visits: honest information only

7.1 Use and share only official sources for rules, fees and procedures (for example TISEZA, BRELA, TRA, the immigration department and ministry websites). Do not quote fees, timelines or "insider" procedures you cannot show from an official source.

7.2 Arrange meetings only with people and organisations the client asks to meet, or with organisations that have a public office and address. Never present a meeting as "official" or "approved by government" when it is not.

7.3 Do not tell a client that Kwetuly has checked a project, partner, land or company. Kwetuly does not check whether a project is a good investment.

7.4 If you interpret in a meeting, interpret fully and neutrally. Say so if you do not understand a technical or legal term. Do not interpret contracts or legal documents as if you were a lawyer; suggest the client uses a qualified advocate and a certified translator.

7.5 Never sign anything, pay anything or give any undertaking on behalf of a client.

### 8. VIP trips: privacy and safety, not security

8.1 Plan for safety in the normal way: licensed guides and vehicles, safe stays, first aid, weather, park rules, emergency contacts and the nearest hospital.

8.2 You are not a security guard or bodyguard. If a client needs security, tell them to engage a licensed security provider directly. Kwetuly does not arrange or recommend one. **[Advocate]** to confirm the wording.

8.3 If a client brings their own security team, cooperate on timings and routes, but do not take orders to break the law or this code, and do not carry or handle weapons.

8.4 Do not share a VIP client's route or timetable with anyone who does not need it.

### 9. Honest profiles and pricing

9.1 Describe your host service truthfully: what you do, languages, areas, group size, what is and is not included, and your day rate.

9.2 State only experience, licences and qualifications you really have. A licence or certificate shows on your profile only if Kwetuly has seen it, and the profile says what was checked and when. Never say or suggest that Kwetuly recommends, certifies or guarantees you. Do not use words like "VIP security", "investment expert", "government-connected" or "fast-track".

9.3 Charge only the agreed price. Give the client a clear written breakdown and receipt. Do not add hidden mark-ups on third-party costs; if you add a fee, say so. You are responsible for your own tax. **[Advocate]** to confirm receipt and tax rules.

9.4 Reviews: do not write your own, ask friends to write them, pay for them or pressure clients.

### 10. Complaints and reports

10.1 Clients, hosts, guides, crew and community members can report a host, a trip, a message or a person with the report button in Kwetuly, or by writing to [SAFETY CONTACT].

10.2 Hosts must report within [24 hours]:
- any request for or offer of a bribe;
- harassment, threats, violence or sexual behaviour;
- an accident, injury or health emergency on a trip;
- a leak or loss of client information, or a lost or stolen device holding it;
- a scam, fake investment offer or someone pretending to be Kwetuly or an official;
- a client asking for something illegal.

10.3 In an emergency, contact the emergency services first, then Kwetuly.

10.4 Say what happened, when, and which booking it was. Do not attach ID documents, passport pages, health details or private client documents. Kwetuly will ask for what it needs.

10.5 Kwetuly will confirm it received a report within [2 working days], keep it private to the people who need to handle it, and tell the person who reported the outcome where it can. No one may be punished for reporting in good faith. False reports made on purpose break this code.

10.6 If a client complains about you, Kwetuly will tell you what the complaint is about (without exposing the person who reported where that would put them at risk) and ask for your side, usually within [7] days.

### 11. Steps if the rules are broken, and removal

11.1 Kwetuly looks at the facts, tells you what was reported and gives you a chance to explain, unless there is an urgent safety risk.

11.2 Depending on how serious it is, Kwetuly may:
1. **Reminder or written warning** – for minor or first problems (for example late once, unclear service description).
2. **Hide the host service** until a fix is made – for misleading descriptions, unclear pricing, repeated lateness.
3. **Suspend** the host service or whole profile while Kwetuly looks into a serious report.
4. **Remove** the host service or remove the person from Kwetuly, and remove any checks shown on the profile.

11.3 **Immediate suspension** (step 3 straight away) for: offering or paying a bribe; sharing confidential client information or posting a client without consent; giving investment advice or brokering a deal; undisclosed commissions or side deals; acting as security or carrying a weapon; sexual behaviour or harassment; threats or violence; fraud or pretending to be Kwetuly or an official.

11.4 Walter makes every suspension and removal decision. Kwetuly keeps a private record of reports and decisions, with no more personal data than needed. **[Advocate]** to confirm the fairness of this process, how long records are kept, and how it fits with the Terms for Guides.

11.5 You may ask Walter to review a decision once, in writing, within [14] days.

11.6 **When you stop being a host** (by your choice or Kwetuly's): finish or hand over any confirmed trips as Kwetuly agrees with the client; delete client information you still hold, except what the law requires you to keep; stop using the Kwetuly name; your confidentiality duty (section 4) continues.

### 12. Your agreement

By ticking "I accept the host rules" you confirm you have read this code, will follow it, and understand that breaking it can lead to the steps in section 11.

# Kanuni za Maadili za Kwetuly kwa Wenyeji wa Safari (wenyeji wa safari binafsi za VIP na wenyeji wa ziara za uwekezaji)

### 1. Kanuni hizi ni za nani

1.1 Kanuni hizi zinamhusu kila mwongozaji wa Kwetuly anayetoa huduma ya mwenyeji wa safari binafsi ya VIP au mwenyeji wa ziara ya uwekezaji ("wewe", "mwenyeji"), na kila safari, mkutano, ujumbe na mawasiliano na mteja yanayoanzia Kwetuly.

1.2 Wewe ni mwenyeji huru, si mfanyakazi wa Kwetuly. Unaamua bei yako ya siku, mipango na ratiba ndani ya kanuni hizi. **[Wakili]** Ihakikishwe kama kanuni na mamlaka ya Kwetuly yanaweza kuwafanya wenyeji waonekane kama wafanyakazi chini ya Sheria ya Ajira na Mahusiano Kazini.

1.3 Mteja akikuweka nafasi, makubaliano ya huduma ni kati yako na mteja. **[Wakili]** athibitishe nani ni muuzaji, na kama kupanga safari nzima kunahitaji leseni ya wakala wa utalii au wakala wa usafiri badala ya leseni ya mwongozaji peke yake.

### 2. Mwenyeji anafanya nini, na asichopaswa kufanya kamwe

2.1 Mwenyeji anaweza: kupanga safari au ratiba ya ziara; kuweka au kupendekeza waongozaji, malazi na shughuli zenye leseni; kuambatana na mteja; kueleza mila za eneo; kutafsiri kama ana ufasaha; kupanga mikutano ambayo mteja ameomba; na kuonyesha tovuti na ofisi rasmi.

2.2 Mwenyeji **kamwe** asi:
- toe ushauri wa uwekezaji, fedha, kodi au sheria, wala kusema kama mradi, kampuni, ardhi au hisa ni uwekezaji mzuri;
- ahidi kwamba leseni, kibali, hati ya ardhi, viza, kibali cha kazi au usajili utaidhinishwa, au utaidhinishwa haraka zaidi;
- toe, kulipa, kuomba au kupitisha rushwa, "ada ya kuharakisha" au zawadi kwa afisa yeyote wa umma, polisi, askari wa hifadhi, afisa wa mpakani au mfanyakazi wa taasisi ya serikali;
- shike, kupokea au kuhamisha fedha za uwekezaji za mteja, wala kumwakilisha mbele ya ofisi ya serikali bila mamlaka ya maandishi na leseni inayofaa;
- toe au kufanya kazi ya ulinzi, mlinzi binafsi (bodyguard), kubeba silaha, au kujiita "ulinzi";
- mwendeshe mteja kama usafiri wa kulipiwa kupitia Kwetuly (usafiri umezimwa);
- panga jambo lolote lisilo halali, ikiwemo uwindaji bila leseni, bidhaa za wanyamapori, dawa za kulevya au huduma za ngono.

2.3 Mteja akiomba lolote kati ya hayo ya 2.2, kataa kwa adabu na umwelekeze kwa mtaalamu au chanzo rasmi kinachofaa.

2.4 **[Wakili]** athibitishe: leseni anayohitaji mwenyeji wa VIP; kama kazi ya ulinzi inadhibitiwa na vipi; nani anaweza kumwakilisha mwekezaji mbele ya TISEZA, BRELA au taasisi nyingine; na kama huduma ya ziara ya uwekezaji inahitaji chochote kutoka Mamlaka ya Masoko ya Mitaji na Dhamana (CMSA).

### 3. Tabia ya kitaaluma

3.1 Fika kwa wakati, ukiwa umejiandaa, bila kulewa na umevaa ipasavyo kwa maeneo mnayotembelea.

3.2 Mheshimu kila mteja, mwongozaji, mbeba mizigo, dereva, afisa na mwanajamii, bila kujali nchi, umri, jinsia, dini, asili, ulemavu au sifa nyingine yoyote binafsi.

3.3 Usinywe pombe wala kutumia dawa za kulevya ukiwa kazini.

3.4 Hakuna maneno, utani, ishara, maombi au mahusiano ya kingono na wateja wakati wewe ni mwenyeji wao.

3.5 Watendee jamii haki: omba ruhusa kabla ya kupiga picha watu, fuata kanuni za vijiji na maeneo, na usiwapeleke wateja majumbani au vijijini bila ridhaa ya jamii.

3.6 Walipe watu unaowaleta (waongozaji, wabeba mizigo, wapishi, madereva, wakalimani) kwa haki, kwa wakati na angalau [viwango vya mwongozo wa Kwetuly / kima cha chini cha kisheria – Walter aamue]. Usichukue bakshishi zao.

### 4. Usiri wa taarifa za mteja

4.1 Kila unachojua kuhusu mteja ni **siri**: yeye ni nani, kwamba yuko Tanzania, anakolala, mipango na ratiba yake, anaokutana nao, maslahi yake ya biashara, fedha, familia, afya na hati za safari.

4.2 Usi:
- mwambie mtu yeyote mteja wako ni nani au yuko wapi;
- chapishe picha, video, mahali au "check-in" za mteja, gari lake, hoteli au mikutano yake bila **ridhaa yake ya maandishi tofauti**;
- shiriki mipango ya biashara ya mteja au majina ya anaokutana nao na mtu yeyote;
- hifadhi nakala za pasipoti, vitambulisho au hati nyingine; zikihitajika kwa nafasi, zitume moja kwa moja kwenye mfumo rasmi au biashara yenye leseni, kisha futa nakala yako;
- jadili wateja kwenye magrupu ya mazungumzo.

4.3 Washirikishe waongozaji, malazi na madereva tu kile wanachohitaji kwa sehemu yao ya safari.

4.4 Tumia ujumbe wa Kwetuly kupanga. Linda simu na kompyuta yako kwa nywila au PIN. Simu ikipotea au kuibiwa, au ukidhani taarifa za mteja zimevuja, iambie Kwetuly ndani ya [saa 24].

4.5 Usiri unaendelea baada ya safari kuisha, na baada ya kuacha kuwa mwenyeji wa Kwetuly.

4.6 Usiri haukuzuii kuripoti uhalifu, hatari ya usalama au uvunjaji wa kanuni hizi kwa Kwetuly, wala kufanya kile sheria inachotaka.

4.7 **[Wakili]** Wenyeji wanaweza kuwa na wajibu wao wenyewe chini ya Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi, 2022; wakili athibitishe, na kama mkataba tofauti wa usiri (NDA) unahitajika.

### 5. Hakuna zawadi, mikataba ya pembeni wala mgongano wa maslahi

5.1 **Zawadi kutoka kwa wateja.** Unaweza kupokea shukrani ndogo ya hiari au bakshishi isiyozidi [USD 50 / Walter aamue] inayotolewa wazi. Usiombe zawadi, mikopo, pesa za familia, ada za shule, msaada wa viza au uwekezaji katika miradi yako. Ripoti zawadi yoyote inayozidi kiwango hicho na uikatae.

5.2 **Zawadi na kamisheni kutoka kwa wengine.** Usipokee pesa, zawadi, kamisheni au "mlungula" kutoka hoteli, maduka, wauzaji ardhi, wamiliki wa miradi, mawakili, mawakala au mtu yeyote kwa kumpeleka mteja kwao, isipokuwa mpango huo umeidhinishwa na Kwetuly na **mteja ameambiwa kwa maandishi kabla** ya kuamua.

5.3 **Mikataba ya pembeni.** Usi:
- mtoe mteja nje ya Kwetuly ili kukwepa kanuni, kamisheni au maoni ya Kwetuly **[Wakili]** athibitishe kizuizi hiki ni cha haki;
- uze au kuwa dalali wa ardhi, nyumba, madini, vito, hisa za kampuni au uwekezaji wowote kwa mteja, wala kumtambulisha kwa mpango ambao wewe, familia au marafiki zako mna maslahi nao;
- we mbia, mfanyakazi au wakala wa biashara ya mteja wakati wewe ni mwenyeji wake.

5.4 **Mgongano wa maslahi.** Wewe, familia yako au rafiki wa karibu mkiwa na maslahi yoyote katika biashara, mradi, ardhi au mtu ambaye mteja anataka kumtembelea, mwambie mteja na Kwetuly kwa maandishi kabla ya ziara.

### 6. Hakuna malipo kwa maafisa

6.1 Kamwe usitoe, kutoa ahadi, kuomba wala kupokea rushwa kwa namna yoyote (pesa taslimu, pesa za simu, zawadi, chakula, fadhila, ajira), kwa au kutoka kwa afisa yeyote wa umma.

6.2 Lipa tu ada rasmi zilizotangazwa, kupitia njia rasmi, na uombe risiti rasmi.

6.3 Afisa akikuomba wewe au mteja malipo yasiyo rasmi: kataa kwa adabu, usilipe, mweke mteja salama na mtulivu, andika muda, mahali na kilichoombwa (bila taarifa binafsi za mteja), na uripoti kwa Kwetuly ndani ya [saa 24]. Mtu yeyote akiwa hatarini, usalama kwanza.

6.4 **[Wakili]** athibitishe wajibu chini ya Sheria ya Kuzuia na Kupambana na Rushwa na wajibu wowote wa kuripoti kwa TAKUKURU. Ni Walter tu anayeamua lini Kwetuly iwasiliane na mamlaka.

### 7. Ziara za uwekezaji: taarifa za kweli tu

7.1 Tumia na shiriki vyanzo rasmi tu kwa kanuni, ada na taratibu (kwa mfano TISEZA, BRELA, TRA, Idara ya Uhamiaji na tovuti za wizara).

7.2 Panga mikutano tu na watu na taasisi ambazo mteja ameomba kukutana nazo, au taasisi zenye ofisi na anwani ya wazi. Usiuonyeshe mkutano kama "rasmi" au "umeidhinishwa na serikali" kama sivyo.

7.3 Usimwambie mteja kwamba Kwetuly imekagua mradi, mbia, ardhi au kampuni. Kwetuly haikagui kama mradi ni uwekezaji mzuri.

7.4 Ukitafsiri kwenye mkutano, tafsiri kikamilifu bila upendeleo. Usitafsiri mikataba au hati za kisheria kana kwamba wewe ni mwanasheria; mshauri mteja atumie wakili na mtafsiri aliyethibitishwa.

7.5 Kamwe usisaini, kulipa au kutoa ahadi yoyote kwa niaba ya mteja.

### 8. Safari za VIP: faragha na usalama wa kawaida, si ulinzi

8.1 Panga usalama kwa njia ya kawaida: waongozaji na magari yenye leseni, malazi salama, huduma ya kwanza, hali ya hewa, kanuni za hifadhi, mawasiliano ya dharura na hospitali iliyo karibu.

8.2 Wewe si mlinzi. Mteja akihitaji ulinzi, mwambie aajiri mtoa huduma ya ulinzi mwenye leseni moja kwa moja. Kwetuly haipangi wala haipendekezi. **[Wakili]** athibitishe maneno haya.

8.3 Mteja akija na timu yake ya ulinzi, shirikiana kuhusu muda na njia, lakini usipokee maagizo ya kuvunja sheria au kanuni hizi, na usibebe wala kushika silaha.

8.4 Usishiriki njia au ratiba ya mteja wa VIP na mtu yeyote asiyehitaji kuijua.

### 9. Wasifu na bei za kweli

9.1 Eleza huduma yako kwa ukweli: unachofanya, lugha, maeneo, ukubwa wa kikundi, kilichojumuishwa na kisichojumuishwa, na bei yako ya siku.

9.2 Taja uzoefu, leseni na sifa ulizo nazo kweli tu. Leseni au cheti kinaonekana kwenye wasifu wako tu kama Kwetuly imekiona, na wasifu unaonyesha kilichokaguliwa na lini. Usiseme wala kudokeza kwamba Kwetuly inakupendekeza au inakuhakikishia. Usitumie maneno kama "ulinzi wa VIP", "mtaalamu wa uwekezaji", "ana uhusiano na serikali" au "njia ya haraka".

9.3 Toza bei iliyokubaliwa tu. Mpe mteja mchanganuo wa maandishi na risiti. Usiongeze faida iliyofichwa kwenye gharama za watu wengine. Unawajibika kwa kodi yako mwenyewe. **[Wakili]** athibitishe kanuni za risiti na kodi.

### 10. Malalamiko na ripoti

### C2. Interview questions (45 minutes, same questions for every candidate)

1. Tell us about trips you have organised for private or high-profile clients, or business visitors. What was your role? (Do not name clients.)
2. A client is a well-known person. A friend of yours who works at a hotel asks you who you are hosting this week. What do you say?
3. A client asks you to take photos of them at the meeting and post them on your Instagram to "show the trip". What do you do?
4. At a road checkpoint, an officer suggests that a small payment would make things faster. Your client is in a hurry. What do you do, step by step?
5. An investment client asks you: "Is this farm land a good buy? You know the area." How do you answer?
6. A land seller offers you 5% if your client buys. What do you do?
7. A VIP client asks you to act as their bodyguard for the evening and offers extra pay. What do you say?
8. A client offers you a gift worth about USD 300 at the end of the trip. What do you do?
9. A client wants to meet "someone at the ministry who can speed up the permit". How do you handle it?
10. You realise your cousin owns the company your client wants to visit. What do you do?
11. Your phone with the client's trip plan is stolen in town. What do you do?
12. How do you make sure the porters, cooks and drivers on your trips are paid fairly?
13. What questions do you have about Kwetuly or these rules?
